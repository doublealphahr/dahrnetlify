Candidate Privacy Notice

Last updated: 12 October 2026  |  Effective: 12 October 2026

This Candidate Privacy Notice explains how Double Alpha HR Consulting ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal data when you engage with us as a candidate for permanent employment, or when you are contacted by us about work-related opportunities.

It should be read together with our General Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

Quick summary: We process your CV and application data to assess your suitability for roles and to contact you about opportunities. We do not sell your data. We will only share your CV with a client if you have agreed we may do so.

1. Who This Notice Applies To

This notice applies to you if you engage with us as a candidate for permanent employment, or if your profile or application has been introduced to us by another recruitment supplier, agency, or similar intermediary.

If you are a client or supplier of Double Alpha HR Consulting, please refer to our General Privacy Policy.

3. What Personal Data We Collect

We only collect personal data that is necessary to engage with you in connection with work-related opportunities, including the assessment of your profile and, where applicable, the initiation and administration of any resulting contractual relationship.

3.1 Personal data you provide directly

  • Name and contact details (email address, phone number, postal address)
  • CV/resume, work history, qualifications, education
  • Skills, certifications, professional memberships
  • Current and expected remuneration
  • Cover letters, questionnaires, assessment responses
  • Other information you choose to share with us (such as nationality, right-to-work status, or references)

3.2 Personal data we collect indirectly from other sources

  • References and employment verification, where you have provided referee details
  • Your published professional profile (e.g., LinkedIn), where you have made it publicly available for professional purposes
  • CV or employment details provided to us by another recruitment agency, where you have authorised them to do so

3.3 Sensitive or special category data

We do not routinely collect special category data (such as health, ethnicity, religious beliefs, or sexual orientation). If you voluntarily provide such information (for example, in connection with a request for reasonable adjustments during the recruitment process), we will process it only with your explicit consent or where otherwise permitted by law.

3.4 Personal data we collect automatically

Our Site uses only strictly necessary cookies. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. For details, see our Cookie Policy.

4. Where We Obtain Personal Data From

In most cases, we collect your personal data directly from you — for example when you:

  • Submit your CV through our Site or by email;
  • Apply for a role advertised by us;
  • Contact us about work-related opportunities.

We may also receive personal data about you from:

  • Recruitment suppliers, agencies, or intermediaries who introduce your profile to us;
  • Referees or former employers, where you have asked them to share information with us;
  • Publicly available professional sources, such as LinkedIn, where you have made your profile available for recruitment purposes.

6. Candidate Pool

If you have submitted your CV to us, we may retain it in our candidate database so that we can consider you for current and future roles that match your skills and experience. If we find a role that may be suitable, we will contact you to check whether you are interested.

If you do not wish to be part of our candidate pool, you can ask us to remove your details at any time — see Section 8.

8. Your Rights

Under the UK/EU GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion (subject to lawful exceptions)
  • Restrict certain processing
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at Business@doublealphahr.com. We will respond within one month.

9. How Long We Retain Your Personal Data

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected:

  • CVs and job applications: up to 12 months from the date we last had meaningful contact with you, unless you ask us to retain them longer or we have an ongoing opportunity.
  • If you ask us not to keep your details in our candidate pool: we will only retain your data for as long as we need to comply with legal and regulatory requirements.
  • Recruitment records: kept for the period required by law.

10. International Transfers

Our Site and systems are hosted on servers located in [country/region]. If we transfer your personal data outside the UK or EEA, we will ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

11. Automated Decision-Making and AI

We do not use fully automated decision-making systems to make hiring decisions about you. Any tools we use are designed to support human decision-making only. All final recruitment decisions are made by our consultants with human review.

12. How We Protect Your Personal Data

We are committed to protecting your personal data. We use appropriate organisational and technical security measures, including:

  • Encrypted transmission of data over HTTPS
  • Role-based access controls
  • Secure data storage
  • Regular security reviews
  • Staff training on data protection and confidentiality
