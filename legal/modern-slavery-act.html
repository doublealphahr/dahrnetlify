Modern Slavery & Human Trafficking Statement

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Slavery and human trafficking are human rights abuses and have no place in our business or in our supply chain. In line with our values, we are dedicated to operating our business responsibly and committed to the highest standards of conduct.

Our Statement

This statement has been published in accordance with the UK Modern Slavery Act 2015 (the "Act") and sets out the steps taken by Double Alpha HR Consulting Ltd. to mitigate the risk of modern slavery within our business and supply chain.

The statement describes the steps we have taken during the current financial year, and the steps we intend to take next year.

Our Business and Supply Chain

Double Alpha HR Consulting provides HR consulting and recruitment services. We work with a limited number of suppliers, including IT, hosting, and professional service providers. Given the nature of our business, we assess the risk of modern slavery in our operations and supply chain as low, but we remain vigilant and committed to ongoing monitoring.

Our Policies

We are committed to:

  • Acting ethically and with integrity in all our business relationships
  • Implementing and enforcing effective systems and controls to ensure slavery and human trafficking are not taking place in our business or supply chain
  • Complying with all applicable laws and regulations relating to modern slavery and human trafficking

Due Diligence

As part of our initiative to identify and mitigate risk, we:

  • Review our supply chain to identify any high-risk areas
  • Require our suppliers to comply with all applicable laws, including the Modern Slavery Act 2015
  • Encourage the reporting of any concerns about modern slavery or human trafficking in our business or supply chain

Training and Awareness

We make our colleagues aware of the risks of modern slavery and human trafficking, and encourage them to report any concerns.

Looking Ahead

We will continue to review our policies and processes to ensure they remain effective in preventing modern slavery and human trafficking in our business and supply chain.

Approval

This statement has been approved by the management of Double Alpha HR Consulting Ltd. and will be reviewed annually.
