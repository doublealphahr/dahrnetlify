Website FAQs

General issues

If you're experiencing problems using the Double Alpha HR website, here are a few tips which could help:

  • Check that you're using a supported browser. We recommend the latest versions of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari.
  • Check that your web browser has cookies enabled. See your browser's help documentation for guidance.
  • If you're using a computer at work, your company may have firewall restrictions that block some functionality. Contact your IT department for further information.

How can I update my details with Double Alpha HR?

Please email us at Business@doublealphahr.com with the details you would like to update.

I visited the site recently, but have issues today with pages not displaying as expected

There may have been a recent update to the website, which can require you to clear your browser cache. See your browser's help documentation for how to do this.

I would like my details removed from doublealphahr.com

Please email us at Business@doublealphahr.com with your request, and we will process it in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Job Seeker FAQs

How do I search for jobs?

Visit our job search page to browse current vacancies. You can also submit your CV to us and we will contact you about roles that match your skills and experience.

How do I apply for jobs?

When you find a role you're interested in, submit your CV through the listing or email us at Business@doublealphahr.com. One of our consultants will be in touch if your profile matches the requirements.

How do I get better search results?

Try using different keywords, exploring different industries or professions, or widening your search area.

I'm having problems submitting my CV

The most common issues when uploading a CV are format and file size. Please ensure:

  • The format is .doc, .docx, .pdf, .rtf, or .txt
  • The file format is in lower case (e.g., .doc, not .DOC)
  • The file size is below 5 MB
