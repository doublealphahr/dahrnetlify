Our approach to DEI & Wellbeing

Diversity, Equity, Inclusion and Wellbeing are integral to everything we do — shaping a better future for ourselves, our colleagues, our clients, and the candidates we support. We're building a culture that empowers people to be bold, curious, and collaborative, where every voice is valued and every person is included.

Our Commitment to Mental Health and Wellbeing

Mental health is a cornerstone of our wellbeing strategy and a vital part of our inclusive culture. We believe that inclusion is essential to wellbeing, and wellbeing is essential to performance.

We're committed to enabling colleagues to thrive by supporting their physical, mental, social, and financial wellbeing. Our approach is built on:

  • Good work autonomy — trusting people to manage their work in the way that suits them best
  • Fair pay — ensuring equitable compensation for equivalent work
  • Purpose — helping every colleague understand how their work contributes
  • Growth — supporting learning and development at every career stage
  • Healthy work-life balance — respecting boundaries and supporting flexibility

A Culture of Psychological Safety

We strive to create a psychologically safe environment where it's okay to talk about mental health, free from judgement or stigma. Wellbeing is a shared responsibility, and we all play a part in creating a workplace where people feel supported, valued, and able to bring their authentic selves to work.

We support open conversations about disability, neurodivergence, mental health, and other health challenges, and we work with our colleagues to agree on any workplace adjustments they may need.

Empowering Through Leadership

Strong leadership is at the heart of our approach. We give our leaders and managers the tools, training, and confidence to support mental health and to foster inclusive teams.

  • Manager training: helping leaders understand their influence on team wellbeing and develop skills for compassionate conversations.
  • Menopause awareness: raising awareness of menopause in the workplace and supporting colleagues affected by it.
  • Mental Health First Aiders: a network of trained colleagues who help normalise conversations about mental health, encourage early intervention, and provide safe, judgement-free spaces for support.

Inclusive and Holistic Support

We're committed to supporting colleagues' wellbeing in every aspect of life. By working with trusted partners, we help colleagues access the right support, whenever it's needed — ensuring everyone can get help that truly makes a difference.

Measuring Our Impact

We're committed to making a real difference for our colleagues and track our impact using both qualitative and quantitative data. We review our progress regularly and use what we learn to shape our priorities for the year ahead.
