General Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026  |  Effective: 12 October 2026

Double Alpha HR Consulting ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This General Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website doublealphahr.com (the "Site") and when you engage with our HR consulting and recruitment services.

We operate in the United Kingdom and the European Union, and we comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

Quick summary: We collect personal information only when you voluntarily provide it to us — for example, when you submit a CV, contact us, or engage our services. Our Site uses only strictly necessary cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number, and any other details you submit through our contact forms.
  • CV and job application data: employment history, education, qualifications, references, and any other information you include when uploading a CV or applying for a position.
  • Business information: company name, role, and business contact details when you engage our consulting services.
  • Correspondence: records of your communications with us by email, phone, or through the Site.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, our web server automatically logs basic technical information for security and operational purposes, including your IP address, browser type, referring page, and pages visited. This data is used solely to keep the Site secure and functioning and is not used for profiling or marketing.

2.3 Information we do not collect

  • We do not use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics).
  • We do not use advertising or remarketing pixels (such as Meta Pixel).
  • We do not use tracking cookies or cross-site tracking technologies.
  • We do not use cookies to remember your preferences or behavior.
  • We do not build behavioural profiles of website visitors.

3. How We Use Your Personal Data

We use your personal data only for the following purposes:

  • Providing HR consulting services — to respond to your inquiries, deliver services you have requested, and manage our business relationship with you.
  • Recruitment — to assess your suitability for current or future job opportunities, and to contact you about relevant roles.
  • Communication — to respond to your emails, calls, and form submissions.
  • Legal compliance — to comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and tax obligations.
  • Website security — to protect the Site against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share your information only with:

  • Professional advisers — lawyers, accountants, and auditors where necessary.
  • Legal authorities — where required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights.
  • Clients (with your consent) — if you are a candidate, we may share your CV with prospective employers only after obtaining your consent.

Our Site is hosted on servers located in [country/region]. If we transfer your personal data outside the UK or EEA, we will ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Contact inquiries: up to 2 years from the last communication.
  • CVs and job applications: up to 12 months, unless you ask us to retain them longer or we have an ongoing opportunity.
  • Client records: for the duration of the engagement plus 6 years, to comply with legal and tax obligations.
  • Server logs: up to 90 days.

8. Your Rights

Under the UK/EU GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — to ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — to ask us to delete your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to restrict processing — to ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability — to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — with a supervisory authority (see Section 11).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at Business@doublealphahr.com. We will respond within one month. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

9. Data Security

We have put in place appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encrypted transmission of data over HTTPS.
  • Access controls limiting who can view personal data.
  • Regular security reviews of our Site and infrastructure.
  • Staff training on data protection and confidentiality.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Site and services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us so we can delete it.

11. How to Complain

If you have concerns about how we handle your personal data, please contact us first at Business@doublealphahr.com so we can try to resolve the issue.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority:

  • United Kingdom: Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)
  • European Union: the data protection authority in your country of residence. A list is available at EDPB members.
