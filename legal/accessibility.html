Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Double Alpha HR Consulting is committed to making our website accessible to all users, including people with disabilities.

Summary: We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA, in line with the UK Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) Accessibility Regulations 2018 and the EU Web Accessibility Directive (EN 301 549).

General Description of the Service

Our services include HR consulting and recruitment support for organisations and individuals. These services are offered online through our website and by email, telephone, and in-person meetings.

How Our Service Works

We help people find suitable positions by matching their skills and experience with open vacancies. We assist with the application process, arrange interviews, and provide support throughout the recruitment journey.

Accessible Design of the Website

Our website is designed to be accessible to all users. We apply the following principles:

  • Clear and simple navigation
  • Descriptive text alternatives for images where appropriate
  • Compatibility with screen readers and other assistive technologies
  • Readable font sizes and colour contrast
  • Keyboard-accessible interactive elements
  • Responsive design that works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices

Current Accessibility Status

We believe our website substantially complies with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. However, we recognise that some pages may still have accessibility gaps. We are actively working to identify and address any issues.

If you encounter any accessibility issue while using our website, please let us know so we can address it promptly.
