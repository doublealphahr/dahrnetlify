Our Social Purpose Pillars

Our four social purpose pillars — Social, Environmental, Commercial and Operations, and Measurement and Governance — guide how we engage with society, clients, partners, and communities, helping us stay focused on doing the right thing.

Social

We invest in partnerships that help people and organisations succeed, creating opportunities and improving lives. Our Social pillar spans charitable giving, volunteering, employability support, and work to remove barriers to employment.

Environmental

Protecting our planet is a priority. We listen to our colleagues and clients, learn from best practice, and take a broad view of sustainability — addressing the environmental issues that matter most to our business and communities.

Commercial and Operations

We take corporate governance seriously and are committed to responsible, transparent business practices that serve our clients and stakeholders. From diverse teams to sustainable supply chains, we align our commercial operations with the interests of those we work with.

Measurement and Governance

To understand and improve our impact, we need to measure it clearly. We track our progress so we can reflect on what's working while staying focused on what comes next: improving lives and making a lasting difference.
