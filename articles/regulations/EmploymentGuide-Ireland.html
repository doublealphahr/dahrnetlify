Minimum Wage, PRSI, Automatic Pension and Termination Compliance

2026 POLICY UPDATE

Minimum Hourly Wage Rises to EUR 14.15
From January 1, 2026, the minimum hourly wage for employees aged 20 and above increases to EUR 14.15. Enterprises should simultaneously update youth proportional tiers, industry wage comparisons and actual working hours tests.

MyFutureFund Automatic Pension Launches
From January 1, 2026, eligible employees and employers each contribute 1.5% of pre-tax wages, with a 0.5% state subsidy. Enterprises should complete pension eligibility identification and avoid duplicate enrollment.

Class A PRSI Rates Increase
From October 1, 2026, employer PRSI for high-income Class A employees increases from 11.25% to 11.40%, and employee PRSI from 4.20% to 4.35%. Enterprises should switch rates according to actual payroll dates and update annual budgets.

Ireland employment compliance in 2026 is simultaneously affected by the minimum wage increase, Pay Related Social Insurance (PRSI) rates that change by payroll date, the MyFutureFund automatic pension, and new contractual retirement age rules. Enterprises cannot calculate costs based only on the employee's annual salary; they also need to confirm employee age, weekly wage tier, industry employment regulation orders, existing pensions and actual working hours.

This guide is intended for Chinese enterprise HR, finance, legal and overseas business teams that recruit, sign contracts, configure payroll, calculate employer costs, use Employer of Record (EOR) services or handle performance, redundancy and departure in Ireland. The data in this article is organized according to 2026 rules; industry Employment Regulation Orders (ERO), Sectoral Employment Orders (SEO), individual employee tax, work permits and high-risk dismissals still require case-by-case confirmation.

3. Ireland Labor Law and Regulatory Framework

Irish labor relations are mainly governed by employment contract terms, minimum wage, working hours, annual leave, equal employment, payment of wages, unfair dismissal, minimum notice and redundancy regulations. The Workplace Relations Commission (WRC) is responsible for labor rights information, inspection and dispute resolution; the Department of Social Protection manages PRSI and MyFutureFund; Revenue manages the PAYE payroll tax system; and the Department of Enterprise, Tourism and Employment is responsible for work permits and labor policy.

Some industries are subject to Employment Regulation Orders (ERO), Sectoral Employment Orders (SEO) or Registered Employment Agreements (REA), which may specify wages, overtime and other conditions higher than national standards. Security, cleaning, construction and other positions should complete industry order judgment before issuing the offer.

The contract employer is responsible for written terms, wages, PAYE, PRSI, Universal Social Charge (USC), automatic pension, leave, working environment and termination procedures. Client managers may provide working hours, performance and project facts, but must not bypass the contract employer to directly issue dismissal decisions to EOR employees.

4. Recruitment, Offer and Onboarding

Recruitment advertisements, screening and interviews must not discriminate based on gender, marital or family status, age, disability, race, religion, sexual orientation, Traveller community identity or other protected factors. Health, identity and work permit data should have restricted access and set retention periods.

General probation period should not exceed 6 months. When extended for employee benefit or special positions, there must be objective grounds, usually not exceeding 12 months; the probation period of a fixed-term contract should also be proportionate to the contract term and nature of work.

Termination during probation, even if the usual unfair dismissal service threshold has not been reached, should have written reason, contract basis and non-discriminatory procedure. After 13 weeks of continuous service, the employer or employee usually needs at least 1 week's notice; if the contract provides for a longer notice period, it should be handled according to the contract.

Pregnancy, protected status, minimum wage complaints, trade union activities, protected disclosures or other statutory rights cannot be grounds for probation termination. Probation extension should record the original end date, new end date and objective reasons.

Most full-time, part-time, temporary, gig and seasonal employees are protected by the minimum wage. The wage reference period is set by the employer, can be one week but must not exceed one month; the test should use the includable pre-tax wages in that period divided by actual working hours.

Basic wage, some shift allowances, bonuses or service charges may be included in the test; overtime premiums, payment in lieu of notice, expense reimbursements and most benefits in kind cannot be used to fill the minimum wage. The 2026 board and lodgings deduction caps are meals EUR 1.27/working hour, accommodation EUR 33.42/week or EUR 4.77/day.

Dublin office example: an employee aged 20 and above working 40 hours per week has a national minimum weekly wage of EUR 14.15 × 40 = EUR 566, annualized at approximately EUR 29,432 based on 52 weeks. This amount is not the market wage for Dublin professional positions; when an applicable industry order exists, the higher standard should be adopted.

Payslips should separately list pre-tax wages, bonus or commission, PAYE, PRSI, USC, pension and net wages. Bonus clauses should state earning conditions, calculation method, departure treatment and clawback rules; employers cannot deny earned amounts merely with "company discretion."

The employer may decide to give paid rest on the public holiday itself, another paid rest day within one month, an extra day of annual leave or an extra day's pay. Employees may still have entitlements when a public holiday falls on a non-normal working day, but the law does not automatically require the next working day off. Good Friday is not an Irish statutory public holiday unless the contract or practice provides more favorable treatment.

Under current WRC position, eligible employees enjoy 5 days of statutory paid sick leave per year, usually paid by the employer at 70% of daily wage, with a daily cap of EUR 110; employees usually need 13 weeks of continuous service and provide a registered medical practitioner's certificate. Maternity leave is usually 26 weeks of basic maternity leave with an additional 16 weeks of unpaid leave; paternity leave is usually 2 weeks; related state benefits depend on PRSI eligibility.

10. Local Employees and Foreign Employees

Local and foreign employees are in principle protected by Irish minimum wage, working hours, annual leave, public holidays, equal employment and dismissal rules. Non-EEA employees must also have a work permit consistent with the employer, position, basic wage and work location.

Employers should verify work rights before onboarding and contract changes. Cross-border remote work by employees may also affect PRSI affiliation, A1 certificate, PAYE, permanent establishment and cross-border data processing; using Employer of Record services will not automatically resolve these obligations.

11. Remote Work, Data Privacy and Employee Records

Remote work is not an automatic right the employer must approve, but employees may make a request in accordance with the law, and the employer should objectively consider it per WRC guidelines and respond within the specified time. Written arrangements should state the main work location, office frequency, equipment, expenses, working hours records, occupational health and safety, confidentiality and cross-border work approval.

Employee data is governed by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Irish Data Protection Act. Health, discipline, PPSN, payroll and work permit data should have restricted access and set retention periods.

Enterprises should keep contract terms, payslips, actual working hours, annual leave, sick leave, performance, discipline, investigation, employee response, pension and termination documents. Long-term work from another country should not be approved without tax, social security, permanent establishment, data and work rights review.

12. Termination of Employment, Compensation and Final Settlement

Ordinary dismissal requires substantively fair reason and procedural fairness. Statutory potential reasons include capability, competence, qualifications, conduct, genuine redundancy, continued employment would be unlawful or other substantial grounds. Summary dismissal should only be assessed after serious misconduct and a fair investigation.

From June 29, 2026, eligible employees whose contractual retirement age is below the State Pension age of 66 may notify their wish to continue working within the statutory window. If the employer refuses, it should reply in writing within 1 month and provide objective, reasonable and necessary justification based on legitimate aims; the earliest contractual retirement date affected by this regime is September 29, 2026.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

What is the minimum wage in Ireland in 2026?
The minimum hourly wage for employees aged 20 and above from January 1, 2026 is EUR 14.15. Age 19 is EUR 12.74, age 18 is EUR 11.32, under 18 is EUR 9.91; when an applicable industry order exists, the higher standard should be adopted.

Do salaried employees in Ireland still need minimum hourly wage testing?
Yes. Employers should test using includable wages divided by actual working hours in a wage reference period of no more than one month; cannot look only at contractual annual salary.

What is employer PRSI in Ireland in 2026?
For ordinary Class A employees with weekly wages above EUR 552, the employer rate is usually 11.25% until September 30 and 11.40% from October 1. Lower weekly wage tiers may apply 9.00% or 9.15%, determined by actual weekly wage and PRSI class.

Which employees need to join MyFutureFund?
Usually employees aged 23-60, with annual income of at least EUR 20,000 and no other payroll pension. In 2026-2028 employees and employers each contribute 1.5%, with a 0.5% state subsidy.

How much annual leave do Irish employees have per year?
Most employees enjoy up to 4 normal working weeks. Annual leave accrues from onboarding and is calculated by the 1,365-hour method, 117-hour method or 8% method; when multiple methods apply, the more favorable result is used.

Is Good Friday an Irish statutory public holiday?
No. Ireland has 10 statutory public holidays in 2026. Good Friday is usually a normal working day unless the employment contract, collective arrangement or company practice provides more favorable treatment.

How is statutory redundancy compensation calculated in Ireland?
When eligible, it is usually 2 weeks' wages per year of service plus 1 week, with a statutory calculation weekly wage cap of EUR 600. Redundancy must be based on genuine position disappearance and cannot be used as a fixed buyout price for arbitrary dismissal.

Can employees be hired through EOR without an Irish entity?
A feasibility assessment can be conducted, but the contract employer, work rights, Revenue payroll, PRSI, MyFutureFund, industry orders, client management boundaries and termination responsibilities must be verified.
