Joint Committee Wages, Social Security, Leave, and Termination Compliance

2026 POLICY UPDATE

Cross-industry minimum monthly income raised
From July 1, 2026, the cross-industry floor for employees aged 18 and over is adjusted to EUR 2,233.61/month; employers should still verify the applicable JC and position level first.

Night work regime relaxed
From June 1, 2026, night work is in principle open to all industries applying the Labor Law, and the general night shift allowance is updated from July 1; employers should adjust scheduling, procedures, and payroll parameters simultaneously.

New contract resignation notice period adjusted
For contracts starting from August 1, 2026, employee resignation notice during the first 6 months is uniformly 1 week; employers should update HR and payroll rules by contract start date.

Belgium is not a market where only one national minimum wage and one uniform social security ratio need to be applied. Wage levels, indexation, year-end bonus, meal vouchers, commuting allowance, working hours, and industry funds depend heavily on the Joint Committee (JC) and collective labor agreement (CBA) corresponding to the employer's main activity. Before issuing an offer, Chinese companies should first confirm work location, employer main activity, employee category, position level, and applicable JC.

This guide is intended for Chinese company HR, finance, legal, and overseas business teams that recruit, sign contracts, run payroll, calculate employer costs, use Employer of Record (EOR) services, or handle employee departure in Belgium. The content is organized per 2026 rules; specific wage index, industry benefits, social security reductions, work rights, and high-risk termination still need to be confirmed case by case.

2. Three Rules to Watch Most in 2026

Cross-industry minimum monthly income raised

From July 1, 2026, when the applicable JC does not stipulate industry minimum remuneration, the cross-industry average minimum monthly income floor for employees aged 18 and over is adjusted to EUR 2,233.61. Companies should still first complete JC and position level judgment, and cannot write this amount as a national uniform minimum wage applicable to all positions.

Night work regime relaxed and allowance adjusted

From June 1, 2026, all industries applying the Labor Law may in principle introduce night work, but employers must still comply with working hours, rest, implementation procedure, and occupational health requirements. If there is no higher industry rule, from July 1, 2026, the night shift hourly allowance under Collective Labor Agreement No. 49 is generally EUR 1.54, and EUR 1.85 for employees aged 50 and over.

New contract resignation notice rule adjusted

For employment contracts starting from August 1, 2026, employee resignation notice during the first 6 months is uniformly 1 week; contracts starting before that still apply the original bracket rules. Employers should configure two resignation notice tables based on contract start date.

3. Belgium Labor Law and Regulatory Framework

Belgian labor relations are composed of mandatory labor law, national collective agreements, Joint Committee (JC) industry agreements, company collective agreements, works rules, employment contracts, and regional rules. The Federal Public Service Employment, Labour and Social Dialogue is responsible for labor law supervision, and the National Social Security Office (NSSO/RSZ/ONSS) is responsible for social security filing management.

The contract employer bears signing, Dimona immediate employment declaration, quarterly social security declaration (DmfA), payroll, employee withholding, employer contributions, workers' compensation, occupational health and safety, leave, discipline, and termination responsibility. The client manager may manage business results, but must not bypass the contract employer to directly change wages, refuse leave, discipline, or dismiss EOR employees.

Companies should judge in the following order: confirm work region and contract employer; identify JC based on main activity and NACE code; determine white-collar or blue-collar employee and position level; verify wage index, working hours, year-end bonus, and industry benefits; then configure social security, tax, holiday pay, insurance, and termination cost.

4. Recruitment, Offer, and Onboarding

Recruitment pages should use language matching the work region, and clearly state work location, contract type, working hours, salary composition, and main benefits. Candidate screening must not be based on nationality, race, gender, age, disability, religion, family status, or other protected characteristics.

The Offer should itemize basic gross wage, indexation mechanism, fixed allowances, bonus or commission, 13th month or year-end bonus, meal vouchers, eco vouchers, commuting allowance, insurance, work location, remote ratio, working hours, and notice arrangement. "EUR 4,000/month" cannot be directly interpreted as complete annual salary cost.

5. Employment Contracts, Contract Types, and Probation

Indefinite-term contracts are the common form for continuous positions. Fixed-term or specific work contracts should in principle be signed in writing before the employee starts work; continuous use is restricted; lack of written agreement or abuse may cause the contract to be deemed indefinite-term. Replacement contracts should state the replaced person, replacement reason, and ending mechanism.

A fixed-term contract does not mean it can be terminated at any time without cost. In principle, during the first half of the contract term and within a window of maximum 6 months, ordinary notice rules may apply once; after entering the second half, early termination without other lawful path generally generates compensation liability.

Ordinary employment contracts no longer have a general statutory probation period since 2014. A contract stating "3-month probation" does not create free termination without notice or compensation. Student contracts and temporary agency work have special probation rules and should be judged using the corresponding contract system.

Ordinary employees in the initial onboarding period are still in a formal employment relationship. Employers should set position goals, training, feedback, and written records from day one; when termination is needed, notice or payment in lieu rules should apply based on continuous service, and discrimination, retaliation, and protected status risk should be checked.

6. Wages, Minimum Wage, and Gross-to-Net Calculation

Belgian wage floor should be judged in the following order: first determine JC based on employer main activity; then determine position level based on duties, seniority, experience, and employee category; check the industry minimum wage table, indexation date, year-end bonus, and allowances valid for the wage period; only when JC does not stipulate industry minimum remuneration, use the cross-industry average minimum monthly income floor.

From July 1, 2026, the cross-industry floor for employees aged 18 and over is EUR 2,233.61/month. This amount is not a uniform minimum wage for all industries and positions. Industry JC wage tables may be significantly higher and may be adjusted multiple times per year under the indexation mechanism.

Using a Brussels white-collar office employee with monthly gross wage EUR 4,000 as an example, this amount is only for cost illustration and does not represent statutory or market minimum wage. Bonus and commission should state acquisition conditions, assessment period, confirmation time, payment date, refund or recovery rules, and departure handling.

Payslips should separately state normal wage, overtime, night shift, public holiday, fixed allowances, in-kind benefits, expense reimbursement, bonus, holiday pay, employee social security, withholding tax, and net wage. Employee actual net wage depends on income, family situation, residence status, benefits, and deductions; companies cannot promise a fixed net wage ratio applicable to everyone.

7. Working Hours, Overtime, and Records

Belgian normal working hours are generally 8 hours/day, 38 hours/week. Industry collective agreements, company works rules, compensatory rest, or cycle averaging systems may adopt different arrangements, but cannot cancel maximum working hours, mandatory rest, and record obligations.

Overtime must have statutory basis and comply with upper limits, approval, record, and compensatory rest requirements. Common overtime surcharge is 50% on workdays or Saturdays, 100% on Sundays or public holidays; applicable JC may provide more favorable treatment. Voluntary overtime also requires prior written agreement and must comply with annual quota and maximum daily and weekly working hours.

From June 1, 2026, night work may in principle be introduced in all industries applying the Labor Law, but employers still need to complete corresponding implementation procedure and occupational health protection. If there is no higher industry standard, from July 1, 2026, employees regularly working between midnight and 5 a.m. generally enjoy EUR 1.54 night shift allowance per hour, and EUR 1.85 for employees aged 50 and over.

Employers should keep scheduling, actual start and end time, rest, overtime approval, and compensation records. Remote employees also need clear working hours, contactable period, and right to disconnect arrangement; fixed salary or manager title does not automatically generate working hours exemption.

8. Public Holidays, Annual Leave, and Other Statutory Leave

Belgian statutory annual leave is mainly formed based on the employee's prior calendar year work record; full qualification for five-day employees is generally maximum 4 weeks or 20 working days. Employees entering employment for the first time, returning after long interruption, or entering Belgium from overseas, after completing at least 3 months or 90 calendar days of activity period, may gradually supplement the current year leave through the supplementary annual leave mechanism.

When a public holiday falls on Sunday or a day the company normally does not work, another ordinary workday must be used as substitute holiday. August 15 and November 1, 2026 may trigger substitute day arrangements, and cannot be directly cancelled because they fall on non-working days.

Eligible ordinary white-collar employees generally have employer guaranteed wage for the first 30 calendar days of sickness; blue-collar employees are connected by employer and mutual insurance system per segmented formula, and white-collar rules cannot be directly applied. Maternity leave is generally 15 weeks; birth leave is 20 days, of which the first 3 days are generally paid normal wage by employer, and the remainder paid by mutual insurance institution per rules.

10. Local Employees and Foreign Employees

Local employees, EU or EEA employees, and other foreign employees in principle enjoy the same Belgian mandatory labor conditions, but work rights, tax residence status, social security coverage, and posting filing may differ. Companies should verify actual work location, permit status, national registration number, or social security identification information before onboarding.

Cross-border posting may require Limosa filing and A1 social security coverage certificate. Employer of Record (EOR) cannot automatically grant a work permit, nor exempt Belgian mandatory wage, working hours, insurance, data protection, or permanent establishment assessment.

Foreign employee departure may affect residence or work permit status; the contract employer should coordinate labor law termination and immigration compliance, but cannot replace lawful termination procedure with permit risk.

11. Remote Work, Data Privacy, and Employee Records

Regular remote and temporary remote arrangements should be documented separately, clarifying main work location, on-site frequency, working hours, contactable period, equipment and expenses, occupational health and safety, information security, and accident reporting. Before an employee works long-term from another country, tax residence, permanent establishment, social security, A1 certificate, data transfer, and work rights should be assessed.

Employer monitoring of email, equipment, or work activity must have lawful purpose, necessity, transparent notice, and appropriate proportionality. Employee health, union, family, and identity data are high-risk data and should have restricted access; medical details should not enter ordinary HR files.

Personnel, working hours, wage, Dimona, DmfA, leave, performance, and departure records should be kept per respective statutory periods; a uniform "permanent retention" rule cannot be used. After departure, system access should be promptly closed and equipment and data handover completed.

12. Termination, Compensation, and Final Settlement

Ordinary employer termination should adopt lawful reason and applicable notice or payment in lieu path. Serious reason immediate termination is limited to serious fault and applies strict dual time limits: generally within 3 working days after becoming aware of serious facts, and formal notice of reason within 3 working days after termination.

For contracts starting from August 1, 2026, employee resignation notice during the first 6 months is uniformly 1 week; after 6 months the official seniority ladder continues to apply, with employee resignation notice generally up to 13 weeks. Longer seniority should consult the complete official notice table.

For example, white-collar employee monthly salary EUR 4,000, annual fixed wage EUR 48,000, continuous service 3 years 6 months, employer chooses immediate termination and pays 13 weeks payment in lieu of notice: example weekly wage is EUR 48,000 ÷ 52 = EUR 923.08, basic payment in lieu approximately EUR 12,000.04.

Final settlement should also add contract benefit value due during notice period, current wage, variable pay, holiday pay, year-end bonus proportion, meal voucher or vehicle benefits, expenses, and tax and social security. C4, holiday certificate, payslip, and payment date should be consistent.

Pregnancy, maternity leave, parental leave, union or employee representative, whistleblowing, discrimination complaint, and other protected status may trigger additional compensation. Fixed-term natural expiry and early termination, ordinary performance termination and serious reason immediate termination must not be mixed.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the 2026 Belgium minimum wage?
Belgium does not have a single figure directly applicable to all positions. Companies should first determine the Joint Committee (JC) and position level based on main activity; only when the applicable JC does not have an industry minimum wage, use the cross-industry floor. From July 1, 2026, the floor for employees aged 18 and over is EUR 2,233.61/month.

What is the basic employer social security rate in Belgium?
The basic employer social security rate for general private for-profit sector is generally 25% of taxable wage. This is not all employer cost, and still needs to add holiday pay, year-end bonus, industry fund, workers' compensation, occupational health, meal vouchers, and other benefits.

How much social security do employees generally bear?
Private sector employees generally bear 13.07% basic social security contribution from gross wage. Low-wage employees may apply work bonus reduction, and actual payslip may also include special contributions and personal income tax withholding.

Can ordinary employment contracts set a probation period?
Generally no. Ordinary employment contracts no longer have a general statutory probation period since 2014; writing "3-month probation" in contract does not create free termination without notice or compensation. Student contracts and temporary agency work have special rules.

How many annual leave days does a new employee have in the first year?
This cannot be simply answered as "none." Ordinary statutory annual leave is mainly formed based on prior year work record; employees entering employment for the first time or returning to work, after meeting at least 3 months or 90 calendar days of activity period, may gradually reach 4 weeks in the current year through supplementary annual leave.

Can public holidays falling on weekends be directly cancelled?
No. When a public holiday falls on Sunday or a day the company normally does not work, another ordinary workday should be arranged as substitute holiday, determined per statutory or industry procedure.

What changed in 2026 for employee resignation notice?
For contracts starting from August 1, 2026, employee resignation notice during the first 6 months is uniformly 1 week. Contracts starting before that still apply the original seniority bracket rules.

Can a company hire employees through EOR without a Belgian entity?
Companies may evaluate a compliant Employer of Record (EOR) arrangement, but must confirm the service entity qualification, contract employer, JC, Dimona and DmfA registration, insurance, work rights, actual command, and termination responsibility case by case.
