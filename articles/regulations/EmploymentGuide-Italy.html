Collective Labour Agreements, Social Security, TFR and Termination Compliance

2026 POLICY UPDATE

Fair Wage Centered on Representative CCNL
2026 wage compliance continues to use the comprehensive economic treatment of a representative CCNL as the key standard. Employers must keep evidence of agreement selection and job level basis; a uniform national hourly rate cannot be used.

INPS Contribution Parameters Updated
The 2026 minimum daily wage parameter for employee contributions is EUR 58.13, and the annual maximum contribution base for applicable persons such as new enrollees is EUR 122,295. Enterprises must update payroll systems, and must not mistakenly call these minimum wage.

TFR Automatic Enrollment Mechanism Implemented
From July 1, 2026, relevant private sector new employees usually have 60 days to choose where their TFR goes; without an explicit choice, they will be automatically enrolled in an applicable supplementary pension under the rules. Employers must complete notification, tracking and record-keeping.

Italy does not have a national uniform statutory minimum hourly wage in euros applicable to all industries and positions. Enterprises must first determine the applicable national collective labour agreement (Contratto Collettivo Nazionale di Lavoro, CCNL) and job level, then calculate wages, 13th or 14th month salary, social security, work injury insurance, severance pay (Trattamento di Fine Rapporto, TFR), leave and notice period.

This guide is intended for Chinese enterprise HR, legal, finance and overseas business teams planning to recruit or manage employees in Italy, covering recruitment and onboarding, employment contracts, payroll calculation, working hours and leave, social security, foreign employees, remote work, termination of employment and Employer of Record (EOR) and other key issues.

3. Italy Labor Law and Regulatory Framework

Italian employment relationships are jointly determined by the Civil Code, labor regulations, representative CCNLs, company or regional agreements and employment contracts.

National Labour InspectorateWorking conditions, employment filing and labor inspection

CCNLs may determine job levels, minimum wages, 13th or 14th month salary, normal working hours, overtime, probation, sick leave, leave, notice period, supplementary healthcare and pension funds.

Enterprises should select the agreement and job level based on main business and the employee's actual duties, and cannot classify based only on English job title.

Fixed-term natural expiry and employer early termination must be handled separately:

The probation period must be in writing, clear and match the employee's actual duties. The specific probation period for an open-ended contract is usually determined by CCNL and job level. If the employee has already been fully validated in the same duties, repeated probation poses an invalidity risk.

During probation, wages, social security, annual leave, TFR, occupational health and safety and anti-discrimination rules continue to apply.

6. Wages, Minimum Wage and Pre-tax Take-home Pay Calculation

Italy does not have a uniform statutory minimum hourly wage in euros applicable to all industries. The wage floor should be determined in the following order:

  1. Determine the applicable representative CCNL based on the employer's main activity;
  2. Determine the category and job level based on the employee's actual duties;
  3. Read the current job table minimum wage and other mandatory fixed economic items;
  4. Confirm 13th month, 14th month, fixed allowances, seniority increments, benefits and regional or company agreements;
  5. Annualize the offer conditions and compare with the CCNL comprehensive economic treatment.

Taking a Milan office position with a monthly salary of EUR 3,000 and 13 annual payments as an example:

Item Calculation Result or Use
Annual gross salary (RAL)3,000 × 13EUR 39,000
12-month budget basis39,000 ÷ 12EUR 3,250/month
13th month monthly accrual3,000 ÷ 12EUR 250/month
Hourly wage referenceCalculated by applicable CCNL divisorCannot uniformly use 173 hours

This example is not a minimum wage conclusion. Before formal quotation, the applicable CCNL and job level wage table must be used to verify whether EUR 39,000 covers all mandatory wage items.

If 14th month salary applies, annual salary, number of payments and monthly accrual must all be recalculated.

Payslips should separately list:

Wage Item Explanation
Basic wageBasic treatment specified by CCNL and job level
Personal superminimumSuperminimo
Seniority incrementCalculated according to CCNL and years of service
Overtime and night workCalculated according to working hours records and CCNL rates
13th or 14th monthAccrued and paid according to CCNL
BenefitsTaxable and non-taxable benefits handled separately
Expense reimbursementSeparate from work remuneration
Social securityEmployee INPS share
Income taxIRPEF and regional, municipal surcharges

Absorbable personal superminimum (superminimo assorbibile) clauses must be clear; future CCNL wage increases cannot be presumed to be offset by default.

Employee gross-to-net must be calculated period by period by the payroll system based on employee INPS share, IRPEF, regional and municipal surcharges, tax deductions, benefits and personal circumstances. Enterprises should not promise a fixed net salary.

Weekly 40 hours is only a general legal starting point. Employers should first verify whether the applicable CCNL specifies 38, 39 or 40 hours, then determine:

  • Part-time additional hours;
  • Ordinary overtime;
  • Night work premium;
  • Sunday work premium;
  • Public holiday work premium;
  • Annual overtime cap;
  • Compensatory rest arrangements.

Whether an employee is a manager cannot be judged only by English job title, nor does it automatically exclude working hours and overtime rights.

Employees bear the INPS share, IRPEF and regional and municipal surcharges listed on the payslip.

The 2026 minimum daily wage parameter for employee contributions is EUR 58.13; the annual maximum contribution base for applicable persons such as new enrollees is EUR 122,295.

These are social security contribution parameters, not statutory minimum wage applicable to all employees.

12. Termination of Employment, Compensation and Final Settlement

Probation termination, ordinary dismissal, summary dismissal, mutual consent termination, employee resignation, fixed-term expiry and fixed-term early termination are different paths and cannot substitute for each other.

Scenario Procedure and Notice Settlement Focus
Employer termination during probationWith valid written probation, usually may terminate for failing probation; must still comply with CCNL, non-discrimination and genuine probation requirementsWages, annual leave, 13th or 14th month proportion and TFR
Employee resignation during probationNotice period determined by CCNL or contractWages, leave, TFR and asset handover
Ordinary dismissalMust have subjective or objective justified reason, stated in writingNotice period or payment in lieu, leave, 13th or 14th month, TFR and dispute risk
Summary dismissalOnly when just cause makes relationship unsustainable; still requires disciplinary procedure and timely written reasonEarned wages, leave, 13th or 14th month proportion and TFR
Mutual consent terminationBoth parties voluntarily determine termination date, amounts and rights and obligations in writing, and complete applicable confirmation procedureStatutory payables and agreed compensation
Employee resignationUsually submitted through official online system; notice period according to CCNLFailure to serve notice period may result in deduction of payment in lieu
Fixed-term expiryEnds on expiry and completes statutory notice and recordsWages, leave, 13th or 14th month proportion and TFR
Fixed-term early terminationOutside probation usually requires just cause or mutual consent or other lawful pathUnlawful termination may generate remaining term loss
Economic or collective redundancyMust have genuine organizational or production reason; reaching threshold triggers union and administrative proceduresSelection criteria, notice period, TFR and litigation risk

Disciplinary dismissal usually requires:

  1. Written specific charges;
  2. Opportunity for employee defense;
  3. Compliance with CCNL and Workers' Statute time limits;
  4. Decision on sanction based on factual severity;
  5. Timely, written statement of final decision.

Italy does not have a uniform ordinary statutory severance formula of "a fixed number of months' wages per year of service."

Employees are in principle entitled to TFR, but unlawful dismissal remedies depend on hire date, company headcount, termination reason and court finding. TFR cannot be regarded as the price for purchasing reason-free dismissal.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Does Italy have a national uniform statutory minimum wage?
There is no uniform euro minimum wage applicable to all industries and positions. Enterprises should usually determine comprehensive economic treatment based on a representative CCNL and job level.

Is Italian monthly salary usually paid only 12 times?
Not necessarily. 13th month salary is common, and some CCNLs also provide 14th month salary, so the offer and budget must specify annual gross salary and number of payments.

Is TFR equal to statutory dismissal compensation?
No. TFR is a deferred wage treatment that is usually settled on termination of the employment relationship and cannot replace lawful dismissal reason, procedure or unlawful dismissal remedies.

Can Italian employer cost be uniformly calculated as wages plus 30%?
No. Actual cost also depends on INPS classification, INAIL risk, TFR, 13th or 14th month, CCNL funds, benefits and applicable reliefs.

Is the EUR 58.13 published by INPS the national minimum daily wage?
It is not a national wage standard applicable to all employees. EUR 58.13 is the 2026 social security contribution minimum daily wage parameter; actual wages must still be determined according to the applicable CCNL and job level.

What happens if an employee does not choose TFR destination after July 2026?
For employees subject to the new rules, if there is no explicit choice within 60 days of onboarding, future TFR is usually automatically enrolled in the applicable collective supplementary pension under the rules.

Does an employee resignation only require sending an email to the company?
Usually not enough. Outside probation, employee resignation generally needs to be submitted through the official online procedure, while complying with the applicable notice period according to the CCNL.

Can using EOR bypass CCNL or automatically resolve work permits?
No. EOR must still correctly apply CCNL and job level, and cannot automatically resolve work permit or immigration quota issues.
