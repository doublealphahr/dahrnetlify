Minimum Wage, Social Contribution Tax, Leave and Termination Compliance

2026 POLICY UPDATE

Two-tier Minimum Wage Increased
From January 1, 2026, the general minimum wage is adjusted to HUF 322,800/month and the guaranteed minimum wage to HUF 373,200/month. Employers should update quotations and payroll parameters based on the objective qualification requirements of the position.

Minimum Social Security Contribution Base Updated
From January 2026, the minimum contribution base for employee social insurance is usually adjusted to HUF 96,840/month. Employers should review low-income, part-time and incomplete-month employees.

Three Sets of Annual Working Day Adjustments Take Effect
In 2026, the general work schedule implements three sets of working day and rest day swaps. Employers should update scheduling, attendance and payroll calendars, and avoid directly applying them to shift workers.

2. Three Rules to Watch Most in 2026

Two-tier Minimum Wage Increased Simultaneously
From January 1, 2026, the general minimum monthly wage is HUF 322,800, and the guaranteed minimum monthly wage for positions requiring at least secondary education or vocational qualifications is HUF 373,200. Enterprises should select the tier based on the objective requirements of the position, pro-rate part-time employees by working hours, and pay overtime and shift supplements separately.

Minimum Social Security Contribution Base Adjusted with Minimum Wage
In 2026, the minimum contribution base for employee social insurance is usually 30% of the general minimum wage, i.e., HUF 96,840/month. Statutory exceptions and incomplete months are adjusted according to rules. Employers should update payroll systems and verify low-income, part-time, onboarding/departure month and special-status employees.

General Work Schedule Implements Three Sets of Working Day Adjustments
January 2, August 21 and December 24, 2026 are adjusted rest days, corresponding to January 10, August 15 and December 12 as working days. This arrangement mainly applies to general work schedule employees; shift or irregular working hours employees must be handled according to actual scheduling.

3. Hungary Labor Law and Regulatory Framework

Hungarian labor relations are mainly governed by the Labor Code, collective agreements, tax and social security regulations, occupational safety, equal treatment and data protection rules. The National Tax and Customs Administration (NAV) is responsible for tax and social insurance filing management. The contract employer bears responsibilities for signing, registration, payroll, withholding, employer contributions, leave, occupational health and safety, discipline and termination.

Enterprises should complete the employment assessment in the following order: confirm the actual work location and contract employer; determine the employee and contract category; identify the minimum wage tier and collective agreement; lock down gross wages and wage components; configure tax, social security and occupational safety; establish working hours and leave records; and re-verify when position, salary or termination changes occur.

In an Employer of Record (EOR) arrangement, the client manager may set business objectives and manage daily collaboration, but must not bypass the contract employer to directly change wages, impose disciplinary action or send dismissal notices.

4. Recruitment, Offer and Onboarding

Job descriptions should clearly state actual responsibilities, work location, reporting line, working hours or shifts, travel, language and objective qualifications. Age, gender, nationality, family status or other protected factors unrelated to the position must not be used to screen candidates.

The offer should separately list basic gross salary, fixed allowances, bonus or commission, overtime arrangements, work location, remote proportion, probation period, notice period and proposed start date. Recruitment platform wages, national average wages or visa wage thresholds cannot replace statutory minimum wage judgment.

Work rights of foreign candidates should be verified separately before onboarding; it cannot be promised that an EOR arrangement will necessarily resolve work permits.

5. Employment Contracts, Contract Types and Probation Period

Open-ended contracts are the common form for long-term positions. Fixed-term contracts usually may not exceed 5 years in aggregate; renewal or re-establishment should have a legitimate employer interest and must not harm the employee; long-term continuous positions cannot repeatedly use fixed-term contracts merely to circumvent protection. Part-time contracts should state weekly working hours and the method of change.

The contract should at least include both parties, start date, work location, duties, wages and payment cycle, working hours, leave, probation period, notice period, applicable collective agreement or policy, bonus or commission, confidentiality, intellectual property, data processing and equipment arrangements. Fixed-term contracts should also state expiry conditions and lawful early termination methods.

The probation period must be agreed in writing, usually maximum 3 months; a collective agreement may extend it to 6 months. During probation, both parties may generally terminate immediately in writing without stating reasons, but still must not be based on discrimination, retaliation or other unlawful reasons. The system should warn before probation expires; after expiry, the probation termination procedure must not continue to be used.

Independent contractor status depends on actual control, organizational integration, economic dependence, substitution rights and business risk. When an individual has fixed scheduling, uses company systems, accepts daily direction and mainly serves a single client, misclassification review should be conducted.

6. Wages, Minimum Wage and Pre-tax Take-home Pay Calculation

From January 1, 2026, the minimum monthly wage for general full-time employees is HUF 322,800; when the position objectively requires at least secondary education or vocational qualifications, the guaranteed minimum monthly wage is HUF 373,200. The applicable tier is determined by the position requirements, not by whether the employee happens to hold a qualification.

The part-time minimum monthly wage may be pro-rated as "full-time wage floor × part-time weekly hours ÷ full-time weekly hours," and reviewed based on actual working hours. Basic salary, shift supplements, overtime pay and public holiday supplements should be tested separately; overtime pay or expense reimbursements cannot be used to fill the basic salary gap.

Taking a Budapest software sales manager with a gross monthly salary of HUF 800,000 as an example, this amount is for cost illustration only and does not represent a statutory or market minimum wage. Bonus and commission should clearly state trigger conditions, assessment period, confirmation time, payment date, cancellation or clawback rules and departure treatment.

7. Working Hours, Overtime and Records

Standard full-time working hours are usually 8 hours/day, 40 hours/week. Working-time Banking may adjust scheduling within statutory limits, but cannot eliminate maximum working hours, rest and record obligations.

Overtime usually uses a 50% wage supplement or compensatory rest; the supplement rate for work on weekly rest days or public holidays may reach 100%, and should be determined according to the type of overtime, scheduling and applicable collective agreement. Annual overtime limits and written voluntary overtime arrangements need case-by-case confirmation.

Employers should keep records of scheduling, actual start and end times, rest, overtime approval and compensation. Whether remote work, business travel, training, standby and cross-time-zone meetings count as working hours should be determined by whether the employee is subject to the employer's direction; a manager title or fixed salary does not automatically create a working hours exemption.

8. Statutory Holidays, Annual Leave and Other Statutory Leave

Employees enjoy 20 working days of basic annual leave per year, and may acquire additional annual leave based on age, number of children, disability or other protected status. Employees do not acquire annual leave only after 12 months of work; the onboarding or departure year should be calculated pro-rata based on the duration of employment.

General work schedule employees in 2026 have three additional working day adjustments: January 2 rest, January 10 work; August 21 rest, August 15 work; December 24 rest, December 12 work. Shift or irregular working hours employees should be handled according to actual scheduling and holiday supplement rules.

Employees usually enjoy 15 working days of sick leave per year, paid by the employer at 70% of absence pay; thereafter, when eligible, they enter social insurance sick pay, and the employer may bear a statutory proportion of the cost. Maternity leave is usually 24 weeks; paternity leave, parental leave and unpaid childcare leave apply their own conditions and payment rules.

10. Local Employees and Foreign Employees

Local employees, EU or EEA employees and other foreign employees in principle enjoy the same minimum labor law protection, but work rights, tax residency and social insurance coverage may differ. Enterprises should verify actual work location, permit status, tax number, social security number and insurance coverage before onboarding.

When cross-border employees hold a valid A1 certificate or other social security coverage certificate, Hungarian contribution obligations may change. Employer of Record (EOR) cannot automatically grant work permits, nor can it eliminate cross-border tax, permanent establishment or co-employment risks.

The departure of foreign employees may affect residence or work permit status. The contract employer should coordinate labor law termination and immigration compliance, but cannot replace lawful termination procedures with permit risk.

11. Remote Work, Data Privacy and Employee Records

Remote work agreements should state the main work location, attendance frequency, equipment and expenses, working hours records, occupational health and safety, information security and cross-border approval. Before an employee works long-term from another country, tax residency, permanent establishment, social insurance, A1 certificate, data transfer and work rights should be assessed.

Employee files should follow lawful basis, necessity, access control and retention periods. Health, discipline, payroll and identity data should have restricted access; system access should be closed promptly after departure and equipment and data handover completed.

Employers should keep contracts and annexes, tax and social security receipts, payslips, working hours, leave, bonus, performance, warnings, accidents, occupational health and termination materials to support payroll calculation and dispute handling.

12. Termination of Employment, Compensation and Final Settlement

Ordinary employer dismissal must have a real, clear and reasonable reason, usually involving employee capability, conduct or employer operations. Summary dismissal applies only to major breach or other circumstances that make the employment relationship unsustainable, and usually must be made within 15 days of learning the reason. During probation, immediate written termination is possible, but discrimination and retaliation must still be excluded.

The basic notice period is 30 days. When terminated by the employer, it increases by years of service: 3 years adds 5 days, 5 years adds 15 days, 8 years adds 20 days, 10 years adds 25 days, 15 years adds 30 days, 18 years adds 40 days, 20 years adds 60 days. The employer should usually exempt the employee from work for at least half of the notice period and continue to pay the corresponding wages.

Statutory severance payment for qualifying employer dismissal is usually: 3 years of service 1 month's wage, 5 years 2 months, 10 years 3 months, 15 years 4 months, 20 years 5 months, 25 years 6 months. Pregnancy, family leave, trade union or employee representative, whistleblowing, discrimination complaints, work injury and disability may trigger special protection.

For example, an employee with a monthly salary of HUF 800,000 and 5 years of continuous service is terminated due to genuine position cancellation: notice period is 45 days, severance payment is 2 months' wage, i.e., HUF 1,600,000; if there are 5 days of unused annual leave, calculated at the example daily base of HUF 800,000 ÷ 21.75, leave compensation is approximately HUF 183,908. Termination compensation subtotal is approximately HUF 1,783,908, plus notice period wages, final wages, earned bonus and expenses.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

What is the minimum wage in Hungary in 2026?
The general full-time minimum monthly wage in 2026 is HUF 322,800; when the position objectively requires at least secondary education or vocational qualifications, the guaranteed minimum monthly wage is HUF 373,200. Part-time employees are pro-rated by working hours.

What are the regular social security costs for Hungarian employers?
In ordinary circumstances, the employer bears a 13% social contribution tax on the wage base. Occupational health, overtime, leave, bonus, collective agreement items and termination costs should be calculated separately; 13% cannot be called the entire employer additional cost.

What wage deductions do employees usually bear?
Employees usually bear 18.5% social insurance and 15% personal income tax, withheld and paid by the employer. Actual net salary also requires applying valid age, family, mother and other personal reliefs.

How long is the probation period in Hungary?
The probation period is usually maximum 3 months; an applicable collective agreement may extend it to 6 months. It must be agreed in writing; during probation, immediate written termination is generally possible, but must not be based on discrimination, retaliation or other unlawful reasons.

Does an employee working less than one year enjoy annual leave?
Yes. Employees do not acquire annual leave only after 12 months of work; the onboarding or departure year should calculate basic annual leave and applicable additional leave pro-rata based on the duration of employment in that year.

Does the 2026 working day adjustment apply to all employees?
Not necessarily. The three sets of working day adjustments mainly apply to general work schedule employees. Employees working shifts, irregular hours or on public holidays should be handled according to actual scheduling and holiday supplement rules.

How much severance payment can an employee with 5 years of service receive when dismissed?
In an employer dismissal meeting statutory severance payment conditions, 5 years of service usually corresponds to 2 months' wage. The 45-day notice period wage, unused leave, earned bonus and compliance expenses should also be calculated separately.

Can employees be hired without a Hungarian entity?
Enterprises can assess a compliant Employer of Record (EOR) arrangement, but must confirm the contract employer, payroll registration, social security, occupational health, work rights, actual management and termination responsibilities case by case.
