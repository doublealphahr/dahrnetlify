Minimum Wage, WPS, Medical Insurance, Pension, End-of-Service Benefits and EOR

2026 POLICY UPDATE

UAE National Minimum Wage Raised: From January 1, 2026, the minimum monthly wage for UAE national employees in the private sector is raised to AED 6,000. Employers should update offers, contracts, payroll systems, and Emiratisation checks simultaneously.

Ramadan Working Hours Reduced by 2 Hours: During Ramadan 2026, daily normal working hours for private sector employees are reduced by 2 hours. Employers should adjust scheduling, attendance, and payroll rules before Ramadan begins, and must not reduce wages as a result.

Summer Heat Stress Prevention Continues: From June 15 to September 15, 2026, direct sunlight and open-air work is restricted from 12:30 to 15:00 daily. Affected employers must adjust scheduling and implement heat prevention, first aid, and HSE documentation.

When hiring employees in Dubai, the first step is not to directly calculate wages or apply for a work permit, but to confirm the contract employer, place of registration, employee nationality, and actual work location. Dubai Mainland, ordinary free zones, and the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) may apply different contract, wage, leave, end-of-service benefit, and dispute resolution rules.

This guide is intended for Chinese companies that recruit locally in Dubai, run payroll, assess Employer of Record (EOR) services, use free zone employment, work remotely, or serve client sites. The content focuses on private sector employment compliance and does not expand on specific visa and work permit application pathways; government employees, domestic workers, and military/police security personnel are also outside the main scope of this document.

Part 4 | Recruitment, Offer, and Onboarding

Recruitment advertisements must not unlawfully discriminate based on race, color, gender, religion, nationality, social origin, disability, or other protected factors. Employers must not pass on recruitment, work permit, or residence costs that should lawfully be borne by the employer to candidates, nor promise that employees can actually work before permit completion.

The contract should clarify total wage (Gross Wage), basic wage (Basic Wage), fixed allowances, payday, probation, 30–90 day ordinary notice, bonus commission, working hours overtime, annual leave, data and IP, and final settlement. Setting basic wage too low will lower EOSB, part of overtime, and unused annual leave amounts, and may trigger disputes over circumventing statutory entitlements.

Probation for Mainland and ordinary free zone employees is generally maximum 6 months, and the same employer must not set it repeatedly. Employer termination during probation generally requires at least 14 days written notice; employee transferring to another employer within UAE generally gives at least 1 month notice; employee leaving UAE generally gives at least 14 days notice. DIFC probation should be handled per its separate law and contract.

Mainland employers should pay wages on time on the contract date, and form a record through WPS or recognized channel. MoHRE guidance treats the first 15 days after wage due date as remediation window, and day 17 may trigger new work permit restriction; internal payment deadline should still be set at contract due date, and 15 days cannot be treated as normal payment period.

Medical Insurance, Unemployment Insurance, and Tax

Dubai registered employers generally should purchase compliant medical insurance for the employee. Unemployment insurance (ILOE) is independent of pension and medical insurance: when basic wage does not exceed AED 16,000, generally AED 5/month plus VAT; when exceeding AED 16,000, generally AED 10/month plus VAT; premium generally borne by employee. UAE generally does not withhold personal income tax on employee wages, but cross-border tax residents, equity, and business income should be assessed separately.

Part 12 | Termination, Severance, and Final Settlement

Before termination, confirm contract type, whether in probation, who initiates termination, genuine reason and evidence, whether employee is in sick leave or maternity leave and other protected status, notice period, service years, unused leave, bonus commission, and whether DIFC or EOR employee. Client project ending itself is not a reason to skip statutory procedure.

After 1 year continuous service, Mainland foreign full-time employees generally have EOSB calculated at 21 days of last basic wage per year for first 5 years, and 30 days per year for the portion over 5 years; fractions prorated, unpaid absence generally not counted as service, total generally not exceeding 2 years wage. All wages and other payables in principle should be paid within 14 days after contract termination.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the minimum wage in Dubai in 2026?
UAE national private sector employees have a minimum monthly wage of AED 6,000 from January 1, 2026. Ordinary foreign employees have no federal uniform minimum wage, but wages still must comply with contract, position, market, free zone system, and other applicable requirements.

Does a Dubai employer need to pay uniform social security for employees?
There is no uniform social security rate applicable to all employees. UAE national employees generally enter GPSSA pension system, other GCC employees are handled per home country extension protection rules, ordinary foreign employees generally have no UAE pension, but need medical insurance and EOSB or alternative savings plan.

Must a Dubai employer purchase medical insurance for foreign employees?
Generally yes. Dubai registered employers generally should purchase and bear compliant medical insurance for the employee; specific scheme, network, and coverage should be determined per authority requirements and contract.

Do ordinary foreign employees need to pay UAE personal income tax?
UAE generally does not withhold personal income tax on employee wages. However, employee tax residence status in other countries, cross-border working days, equity, or business income still need separate assessment.

How many hours per week do Dubai private sector employees work?
Mainland private sector generally 8 hours/day, 48 hours/week. During Ramadan 2026, daily normal working hours are reduced by 2 hours; overtime, night shift, weekly rest, and public holiday work should be recorded and paid separately per applicable rules.

How many annual leave days do Dubai employees have per year?
Mainland employees generally enjoy 30 days paid annual leave per year after 1 year of service; when service exceeds 6 months but less than 1 year, generally 2 days per service month. DIFC employees generally apply separate rule of 20 working days per year.

Can an employee be dismissed same day during probation?
Generally no. Mainland employer termination during probation generally requires at least 14 days written notice, and should complete wage, expense, accrued leave, and permit transition. DIFC should be handled per its separate law and contract.

How is foreign employee end-of-service gratuity calculated?
Mainland foreign full-time employees after 1 year continuous service generally have first 5 years calculated at 21 days of last basic wage per year, and portion over 5 years at 30 days per year. DIFC employees should use DEWS or other qualifying plan rules.

How long after employee departure must payment be completed?
Mainland employers in principle should pay wages and other payables within 14 days after contract termination.
