From January 1, 2026, the ordinary monthly maximum contribution base is adjusted to EUR 6,930, and the special payment annual ceiling to EUR 13,860; employers should update ordinary wage and special payment parameters.

Low-income unemployment insurance relief bracket updated
From January 1, 2026, when employee monthly income does not exceed EUR 2,225, the personal unemployment insurance share is reduced to 0%, with 1%, 2%, or 2.95% applying according to the new income brackets thereafter.

Electronic health card service fee adjusted
The 2027 electronic health card service fee to be deducted on November 15, 2026 is EUR 26.85; employers should configure the payslip in advance and verify applicable employees and exemption situations.

Austria has no uniform statutory minimum wage applicable to all private employees; the wage floor, wage group, salary adjustment, overtime, and special payments usually need to be determined according to the applicable collective agreement (Kollektivvertrag). Before quoting or issuing an offer, Chinese companies should first confirm the employer's industry, employee actual duties, work location, seniority, and collective agreement classification, and cannot treat market salary or position examples as a uniform statutory standard.

This guide is intended for Chinese company HR, finance, legal, and overseas business teams that recruit, sign contracts, run payroll, calculate employer costs, use Employer of Record (EOR) services, or handle performance and departure in Austria. The data in this document is organized per 2026 rules; specific collective agreements, local surcharges, employee personal tax, cross-border social security, and high-risk termination still need to be confirmed case by case.

3. Austria Labor Law and Regulatory Framework

Austrian labor relations are simultaneously governed by statutory labor law, collective agreements, works agreements, individual employment contracts, social security, tax, occupational safety, equal employment, and data protection rules. The Austrian Health Insurance Fund (ÖGK) is responsible for most employee social security registration and contributions, the tax authority manages wage tax, the labor inspectorate is responsible for working hours and occupational safety supervision, and labor courts handle labor disputes.

Collective agreements are the core of wage compliance. Companies should first determine the applicable agreement based on the employer's main business activity, then determine the wage group based on employee actual duties, education and experience, seniority, and work location. Job title, recruitment platform quotation, or visa wage threshold cannot replace collective agreement classification.

The contract employer is responsible for signing, registration, payroll, withholding, employer contributions, leave, occupational health and safety, discipline, and termination procedure. When using Employer of Record services, the client may set business goals and provide performance facts, but must not bypass the contract employer to directly change wages, discipline, or dismiss employees.

4. Recruitment, Offer, and Onboarding

Recruitment advertisements and interview conditions should be relevant to objective position requirements, and must not discriminate based on gender, age, race, religion, disability, family status, or other protected factors. Job advertisements generally need to disclose applicable minimum pay, and may state that the company is willing to provide wages above the minimum standard based on experience.

Employment contracts should at least state parties, start date, work location, duties, normal working hours, wage composition and payment cycle, probation, leave, applicable collective agreement, bonus or commission, notice period, confidentiality, IP, equipment, and data processing requirements.

Ordinary employment relationship probation is generally maximum 1 month, and must be validly agreed in contract or collective agreement. During valid probation, both parties may generally end the relationship immediately without ordinary notice, but discrimination, pregnancy, or other protected reasons may still lead to disputes. Period exceeding 1 month cannot continue to be handled under immediate probation termination rules.

Fixed-term contracts generally end automatically on the agreed date; if ordinary termination before term expiry is needed, the contract should generally explicitly reserve the early termination right. The end of a client project itself does not automatically give the employer early termination right.

6. Wages, Minimum Wage, and Gross-to-Net Calculation

Austria has no uniform national minimum wage. Wage determination should be completed in sequence: determine employer main business and applicable collective agreement, identify employee wage group and seniority bracket, confirm basic wage, verify special payments, overtime, and allowances, and finally configure regular salary adjustment.

13th/14th month pay example: Vienna employee monthly salary EUR 4,000, applicable collective agreement clearly provides holiday pay and Christmas special payment each one month wage, then annual gross contract wage is EUR 4,000 × 14 = EUR 56,000. Company may accrue two special payments at EUR 8,000 ÷ 12 = EUR 666.67 per month, but actual payment time should still follow collective agreement.

If there is no applicable collective agreement, works agreement, or contract basis, two special payments cannot be automatically generated based on local practice alone. For mid-year onboarding or departure, proportional amount, already paid amounts in the year, departure type, and whether recovery is allowed should also be verified.

Employee net wage should be calculated by deducting employee social security and progressive wage tax from gross wage. Personal tax amount is affected by income, deductions, and family situation, so only hypothetical calculation can be provided at recruitment stage, and fixed net wage cannot be promised.

8. Public Holidays, Annual Leave, and Other Statutory Leave

Employees generally enjoy 5 weeks paid annual leave per working year, i.e., 25 days for five-day workers or 30 days for six-day workers. The first working year is prorated for the first 6 months, and after 6 months the employee generally obtains full first-year 5 weeks; thereafter the full year balance is generally obtained at the start of the working year. Recognized service reaching 25 years may increase to 6 weeks, but the inclusion of past service and study years needs case-by-case judgment.

Public holidays falling on weekends generally have no uniform national substitute holiday. Statutory holiday work must fall within permitted situations and be paid holiday wage and applicable surcharge per law, collective agreement, or contract. Good Friday is not a statutory public holiday uniformly enjoyed by all private employees.

Sick pay continuation period generally depends on seniority and prior sick leave within the same working year, and cannot be answered with a uniform "full-pay days per year" for all employees. Mother protection period is generally 8 weeks before and 8 weeks after birth, longer for special births; parental leave, care leave, and related government benefits need separate judgment of employer leave obligation and social security eligibility.

10. Local Employees and Foreign Employees

Local employees and foreign employees are in principle both protected by Austrian collective agreement wages, working hours, annual leave, public holidays, equal employment, and termination rules. Foreign employees must also have work rights consistent with contract entity, position, wage, and actual work location; an offer or EOR arrangement itself cannot replace a permit.

Persons from the EU, EEA, or Switzerland and third-country nationals apply different work and residence rules. Companies should re-verify eligibility before onboarding, renewal, position change, employer change, and work location change, and restrict access to work permit and identity data.

Cross-border employees may also involve social security coverage certificate (A1), tax residence, permanent establishment, wage withholding, and cross-border data transfer. It cannot be assumed that an employee can work long-term from any country just because the employee retains an Austrian contract.

11. Remote Work, Data Privacy, and Employee Records

Remote work agreements should state main work location, on-site frequency, equipment, expenses, working hours records, occupational health and safety, information security, and cross-border work approval. Before an employee works long-term from another country, the company should review personal tax, employer permanent establishment, social security, work rights, and data transfer risk.

Employee data is subject to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Austrian data protection rules. Health, discipline, payroll, identity, and work permit data should be collected per necessity and lawful basis, with access rights, retention period, and deletion process.

Companies should keep employment contracts, collective agreement classification, payslips, actual working hours, overtime, leave, sick leave, performance, discipline, employee response, social security registration, tax filing, and termination documents. After employee departure, system access should be promptly closed and equipment and data recovered.

12. Termination, Compensation, and Final Settlement

Austrian ordinary termination (Kündigung) and immediate termination (Entlassung) are different procedures. Ordinary termination generally does not require the employer to prove serious employee fault, but must still comply with notice period, permitted termination date, employee representative procedure, anti-discrimination rules, and special protection; immediate termination must have a material reason making it impossible for the employer to maintain the employment relationship until notice period expiry, and must be handled promptly.

Contracts may agree termination on the 15th or month-end, and seasonal industry collective agreements may have different rules. Therefore, meeting the notice period length does not mean the employment relationship can end on any date. When a works council (Betriebsrat) exists, the employer generally should inform before issuing ordinary termination notice, and the works council generally has one week to respond; missing the procedure may render the termination invalid.

Departure settlement should include wages up to termination date, notice period wage, applicable proportional special payments, unused annual leave compensation, earned bonus or commission, expenses, sick pay continuation, and statutory documents. Most employment relationships starting after 2003 apply the new severance system (Abfertigung Neu), where the employer regularly contributes 1.53% to the employee severance fund, and at departure the employer generally does not directly pay a fixed number of months again.

Vienna ordinary termination example: employee monthly salary EUR 4,000, continuous service 4 years, contract permits month-end termination and applies 2-month notice period. Notice period wage is EUR 8,000; if applicable collective agreement provides two one-month special payments, two months corresponding accrual is EUR 1,333.33; plus 10 days unused annual leave, budget example calculated as EUR 4,000 ÷ 21.67 × 10 = EUR 1,845.90. Three example items total EUR 11,179.23, but formal settlement still needs to add other payables and deduct already paid amounts.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is there a uniform national statutory minimum wage in Austria?
No. The wage floor for most employees is determined by the applicable collective agreement, wage group, seniority, and work location. Companies should first identify the employer industry and applicable agreement, then determine the position wage.

Does Austrian law require all employees to receive 13th and 14th month pay?
No. 13th/14th month pay usually comes from collective agreement, works agreement, or employment contract. Most collective agreements provide holiday pay and Christmas special payment, so it is very common locally, but amount, calculation base, and payment time are not nationally uniform.

What is the 2026 Austria social security contribution ceiling?
2026 ordinary monthly maximum contribution base is EUR 6,930, and special payment annual ceiling is EUR 13,860. Freelance service personnel and special categories may apply different bases.

How long is probation for ordinary employment relationship in Austria?
Generally maximum 1 month, and should be validly agreed in contract or collective agreement. During valid probation, both parties may generally end the relationship immediately, but anti-discrimination and special protection still apply.

How many annual leave days do Austrian employees have per year?
Generally 5 working weeks. The first working year is prorated for the first 6 months, and after 6 months the full first-year balance is generally obtained; recognized service reaching 25 years may increase to 6 weeks.

Must substitute holiday be provided when Austrian public holiday falls on weekend?
Generally no uniform national deferral substitute holiday requirement. Companies should handle per employee scheduling, applicable collective agreement, and company policy, and cannot directly apply Chinese compensatory leave rules.

Must an Austrian employer prove serious employee misconduct for ordinary termination?
Generally no need to use serious misconduct as prerequisite for ordinary termination, but must meet notice period, permitted termination date, employee representative procedure, and special protection requirements. Immediate termination requires sufficient material reason.

Can a company hire employees through EOR without an Austrian entity?
Feasibility assessment is possible, but contract employer, collective agreement, payroll registration, social security, work permit, actual management, and termination responsibility should be verified case by case. An EOR arrangement cannot automatically resolve all labor, immigration, and tax issues.
