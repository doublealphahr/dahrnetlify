National Minimum Wage, Employer NICs, Pensions, Statutory Leave, Termination and EOR Compliance

2026 POLICY UPDATE

National Living Wage Increased: From April 1, 2026, the National Living Wage for workers aged 21 and over rises to GBP 12.71/hour. Employers should update payroll, contracts, and cost budgets accordingly.

Employer NICs Rate Increased: From April 1, 2026, the employer National Insurance contribution rate rises to 15%, and the secondary threshold is reduced to GBP 5,000/year. Employers should recalculate employer costs and update payroll systems.

Statutory Sick Pay and Family Leave Rates Updated: The 2026 statutory sick pay, maternity pay, paternity pay, shared parental pay, and adoption pay rates are adjusted. Employers should update payroll parameters accordingly.

Hiring employees in the UK means companies cannot rely on a single minimum wage or a fixed employer cost percentage. The UK applies age-banded National Minimum Wage and National Living Wage, and employer National Insurance, pension auto-enrolment, statutory leave, and termination procedures all require separate calculation. Companies should first confirm the employee's work location, contract type, age, wage structure, and employee status, then configure payroll and compliance accordingly.

This guide is intended for HR, finance, legal and overseas business teams of Chinese companies recruiting, signing contracts, running payroll, calculating employer costs, using Employer of Record (EOR) services or handling employee separations in the UK. Amounts in this document are expressed in pounds sterling (GBP); specific rates, thresholds, visa requirements, and foreign employee right to work still need to be confirmed case by case.

3. UK Labor Law and Regulatory Framework

UK labor relations are mainly governed by the Employment Rights Act 1996, National Minimum Wage Act 1998, Working Time Regulations 1998, Employment Relations Act 1999, Equality Act 2010, Pensions Act 2008, and Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992. HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) administers tax and National Insurance, The Pensions Regulator oversees auto-enrolment, and the Advisory, Conciliation and Arbitration Service (Acas) provides guidance on employment relations.

Collective agreements and company policies may set higher standards for wages, working hours, overtime, leave, and termination. Companies should confirm the applicable rules based on industry, company size, and work location.

The contracting employer bears responsibility for employment contracts, payroll, PAYE, National Insurance, pension auto-enrolment, working environment, leave, and termination. Clients or group managers may provide business input but cannot bypass the UK employer to directly discipline or dismiss employees.

10. Local Employees and Foreign Employees

Local and foreign employees in principle enjoy the same labor law protections. Foreign employees must have the right to work consistent with the employer, position, and work location. EOR arrangements do not automatically resolve work visa issues.

Cross-border remote work may trigger tax, social security, permanent establishment, and data protection issues. Employers should assess case by case.

11. Remote Work, Data Privacy and Employee Records

Remote work should be agreed in writing, specifying work location, equipment, expenses, working hours, and data security. The UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 apply to employee data processing.

Employee records should be retained per statutory requirements and include contracts, payslips, working time, leave, and termination documents.

Statutory notice periods in the UK are generally: 1 week for 1 month–2 years of service; 1 week per year of service thereafter, up to 12 weeks after 12 years. Contract may provide longer notice.

Statutory redundancy pay is generally based on age and service: 0.5 week's pay per year for under 22; 1 week's pay per year for 22–40; 1.5 weeks' pay per year for 41+. Weekly pay is capped at GBP 719 (2026). Employers should pay correctly.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the minimum wage in the UK in 2026?
From April 1, 2026, the National Living Wage for workers aged 21 and over is GBP 12.71/hour. Other age bands and apprentice rates are adjusted accordingly. Companies should verify the applicable rate for each employee.

What is the employer National Insurance rate in the UK?
From April 1, 2026, the employer NICs rate is 15% above the secondary threshold of GBP 5,000/year.

How many days of annual leave do UK employees have?
Statutory minimum 5.6 weeks (28 days) per year, including public holidays. Part-time employees accrue pro-rata.

How is redundancy pay calculated in the UK?
Statutory redundancy pay is based on age and service: 0.5 week's pay per year for under 22; 1 week's pay per year for 22–40; 1.5 weeks' pay per year for 41+. Weekly pay is capped at GBP 719 (2026).

Can I use an EOR to hire in the UK?
Yes, but you must verify the contracting employer, right to work, PAYE, pension auto-enrolment, and actual management boundaries. EOR does not automatically resolve all compliance issues.
