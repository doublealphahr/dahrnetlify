Employment Contracts, Compensation and Social Security, Leave and Termination Compliance

2026 POLICY UPDATE

Minimum Wage Fully Unified
The RM1,700 minimum wage has covered ordinary small employers; the transition period no longer applies in 2026.

Foreign Employee EPF Mandatory Contribution
Most applicable foreign employees contribute 2% each by employer and employee, and this must be incorporated into the full-year payroll process in 2026.

LINDUNG 24 Jam Implemented by Employee Category
Local employees participate voluntarily; eligible foreign employees participate mandatorily. The first phase employee rate is 0.75%.

As more Chinese enterprises establish regional teams, sales centers, shared service centers and technical positions in Malaysia, local employment management is no longer just signing employment contracts and paying monthly wages. Enterprises also need to simultaneously handle applicable jurisdiction, minimum wage, working hours, paid leave, Employees Provident Fund (EPF), Social Security Organisation (PERKESO) contributions, Employment Insurance System (EIS), individual income tax withholding and termination of employment procedures.

Malaysia's labor rules have clear regional and employee classification differences. The Employment Act 1955 mainly applies to Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan, while Sabah and Sarawak apply their respective labor ordinances; employee wages, nationality, permanent resident status, age and position nature also affect the application of overtime, statutory contributions and termination compensation. Therefore, enterprises cannot directly use one template to cover all employees.

This guide is intended for Chinese enterprises and HR, legal, finance, payroll and overseas business teams planning to recruit employees in Malaysia, systematically organizing the employment contracts, probation period, minimum wage, working hours and leave, social security, foreign employees, dismissal and retrenchment and Employer of Record (EOR) rules that need to be mastered in 2026, helping enterprises complete the first round of compliance judgment before recruitment, quotation, payroll and employee exit.

2. Three Rules Most in Need of Attention in 2026

2.1 RM1,700 Minimum Wage Has Completed Nationwide Expansion

The Minimum Wage Order 2024 increased the minimum wage to RM1,700/month. For ordinary employers employing fewer than 5 employees and not belonging to professional activity categories, the transition period ended on July 31, 2025, and from August 1, 2025 it must also be implemented.

Therefore, in 2026, the old position of RM1,500 or "small enterprises are still in the deferral period" cannot continue to be used. Domestic servants are not within the coverage of this order.

Payment Method Statutory Minimum Wage

Daily wage for 4-day workweekRM98.08

Employers should re-check the basic wages of low-paid, part-time, piece-rate and pure commission positions, and must not naturally use accommodation, transport, meals, bonuses or other items that do not meet the wage definition to make up the minimum wage.

2.2 Foreign Employee EPF 2%+2% Enters First Full Implementation Year

For most employees holding valid work permits, non-Malaysian citizens and non-permanent residents, mandatory Employees Provident Fund (EPF) has been implemented from the October 2025 payroll, usually with employer and employee each contributing 2%.

2026 is the first operating cycle after the system has run through a complete payroll year. Enterprises can no longer default all foreign employees to "no EPF."

At onboarding, verify the employee's nationality, permanent resident status, age, work permit, existing KWSP membership status and exclusion eligibility. Contributions should usually be completed before the 15th of the following month, and employer share and employee deduction should be listed separately on the payslip.

2.3 LINDUNG 24 Jam Implemented by Employee Category in 2026

The Non-Work-Related Accident Protection Scheme (LINDUNG 24 Jam) is used to protect employees from accidents occurring outside working hours and not directly caused by work.

The first phase employee contribution rate is 0.75%. According to the official status as of July 2026:

Employee Category Participation Requirement Contribution Responsibility
Malaysian local employeesVoluntary participationBorne by employee, withheld and paid by employer
Eligible foreign employeesMandatory participationBorne by employee, employer responsible for registration and withholding

Employers must distinguish local employees from foreign employees in the payroll system, keep local employees' participation or withdrawal status, and continue mandatory withholding for eligible foreign employees. All employees must not be uniformly set to mandatory participation, nor should eligible foreign employees be allowed to freely withdraw.

4. Recruitment, Offer and Onboarding

Before recruitment, determine whether the employee's actual work location belongs to Peninsular Malaysia, Labuan, Sabah or Sarawak, and confirm the applicable labor regulations and public holiday calendar.

At the same time, determine whether the worker is an employee or independent contractor. Fixed attendance, continuous management, organizational integration, use of client equipment and single income source all increase misclassification risk.

Contracts for a specified term exceeding 1 month, or contracts expected to take more than 1 month to complete specific work, should be in written form.

The notice period in the contract should in principle be the same for both parties. If there is no written notice clause, the statutory baseline of 4, 6 or 8 weeks applies according to the employee's years of service.

6. Probation Period Management

Malaysian law does not stipulate a unified 3-month or 6-month probation period cap. The common 3 to 6-month probation period comes from employment contracts and employment practice.

Probationary employees still enjoy basic protection in wages, leave, social security and unfair dismissal. The probation period cannot be regarded as a period during which dismissal without reason is possible at any time.

9.1 Annual Leave

In the year of termination of the employment contract, the period in which the employee has not completed 12 months of service is usually calculated pro-rata by complete service months. Fractions less than 0.5 day are discarded; 0.5 day or more is usually counted as 1 day.

For example, a first-tier employee who departs after 7 complete months:
8 days × 7 ÷ 12 = 4.67 days, calculated as 5 days according to statutory rounding rules.

11. Remote Work, Data Protection and Occupational Safety

Employees may apply for flexible working arrangements under the Employment Act. Employers should approve or reject in writing within the statutory period and state reasons when rejecting.

The remote work agreement should at least state:

  1. Employee's usual location;
  2. Working hours and contactable periods;
  3. Equipment, network and expense bearing;
  4. Information security and accident reporting;
  5. Restrictions on cross-border work;
  6. Re-approval requirements when work location changes.

Before an employee moves to another state or country to work, the enterprise should re-assess public holidays, individual income tax, social security, work permit and corporate permanent establishment risks.

In personal data protection, enterprises should collect only business-necessary data, explain processing purpose, recipients, cross-border transfer, retention period and employee rights, and implement access segregation for identity documents, bank, payroll, medical and disciplinary files.

If the employment contract has no written notice period:

The notice period should in principle be the same for both parties. Payment in lieu of notice equivalent to wages for the unserved notice period may be paid, but payment in lieu of notice cannot replace the just dismissal reason and appropriate procedure the employer should have.

12.3 Retrenchment Procedure and Compensation

Retrenchment, separation schemes, lay-offs or salary reductions should usually be submitted to the Labor Department at least 30 days in advance using Borang PK Parts I to IV. After retrenchment or voluntary separation scheme implementation, Part V should usually be submitted within 14 days, and Part VI within 30 days.

Eligible employees with at least 12 months of service usually have minimum termination or lay-off compensation of:

Years of Service Annual Compensation Standard
Less than 2 years10 days' wages
2 years to less than 5 years15 days' wages
5 years and above20 days' wages

Reference formula:
Retrenchment compensation = wages in past 12 months ÷ 365 × years of service × applicable compensation days.

13. Entity, EOR, PEO and Payroll Operations Selection

Whether to establish a local entity or adopt Employer of Record (EOR) services should be assessed in conjunction with recruitment scale, operation duration, position nature, foreign employee needs, client management degree and cost.

The contract employer should usually bear wages, EPF, PERKESO, EIS, PCB, leave, disciplinary action and termination of employment responsibilities. If the client actually controls recruitment, scheduling, performance and discipline, a clear responsibility matrix and approval process should be established to avoid disconnection between the written contract and actual operations.

Independent contractors must genuinely operate autonomously and bear risk for work results. Long-term full-time work, fixed attendance, serving only a single client, using client tools and accepting daily direction all increase employee misclassification risk.

Foreign employee work permits, quotas, foreign employee levies and sponsorship requirements are not covered in this guide. Adopting EOR does not mean work permit issues can be automatically resolved.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

What is the minimum wage in Malaysia in 2026?
The national minimum wage is RM1,700/month, and the hourly minimum wage is RM8.72. Excluded categories such as domestic servants and higher standards stipulated in contracts or collective agreements should be verified separately.

Can Malaysian employer costs be uniformly calculated as wages plus 12%?
No. Employer costs depend on employee nationality, permanent resident status, age, wages, EPF tier, PERKESO and EIS contribution tables, HRD Corp applicability and company benefits.

Is LINDUNG 24 Jam mandatory?
Malaysian local employees currently participate voluntarily; eligible foreign employees still participate mandatorily. The contribution is borne by the employee, and the employer is responsible for registration and withholding.

Does an employee working less than one year enjoy annual leave?
In the year of termination of the employment contract, proportional annual leave is usually calculated based on the employee's complete service months. The company may also provide a more favorable monthly accrual method in its policy.

Can an employee be dismissed without reason after paying payment in lieu of notice?
No. Notice or payment in lieu of notice is only one element of termination of employment. The employer still needs just cause, supporting evidence and appropriate procedure.

How is retrenchment compensation calculated in Malaysia?
First confirm whether the employee is within the coverage of relevant regulations. Eligible employees with at least 12 months of service are usually calculated at 10, 15 or 20 days' wages per year of service according to years of service.

Are employees with monthly salary exceeding RM4,000 completely unprotected by the Employment Act?
No. The RM4,000 threshold mainly affects certain monetary provisions such as overtime, rest day, public holiday work and termination compensation, and does not mean the employee is completely unprotected by the Employment Act. Manual labor and other specified categories may also continue to apply relevant protections.

Can Chinese enterprises recruit employees in Malaysia through EOR?
It can be assessed, but it needs to be judged case by case in conjunction with position, employee nationality, actual work location, client control degree, work permit requirements and service provider capability.
