Labor Law, Minimum Wage, Social Security, Compensation and Termination Compliance

2026 POLICY UPDATE

Labor Codes Fully Enter Implementation Period
The four Labor Codes have been in effect since November 21, 2025. Employers should complete system updates for contracts, wage structures, payroll, records and termination processes in 2026.

50% Wage Rule Further Clarified
The Ministry of Labour and Employment issued supplementary FAQs on March 16, 2026, explaining the treatment of total remuneration and excluded items. Enterprises with excessively high allowance proportions need to re-test their statutory wage base.

Payroll Tax Switches to New Income Tax Act
The Income Tax Act, 2025 applies from April 1, 2026 for the 2026-27 tax year and subsequent periods. Employers need to update TDS section mapping, forms and payment processes.

Recruiting employees in India in 2026 cannot be done based on a single set of "national uniform parameters." India's labor compliance is simultaneously affected by central Labor Codes, central and state implementation rules, state Shops and Establishments Acts, minimum wage notifications and the employee's actual duties. This guide is intended for Chinese enterprise HR, legal, finance and overseas business teams, using the Delhi office scenario as partial calculation examples to explain the main operational requirements from recruitment, contracts, payroll, working hours, leave, social security to termination of employment.

Enterprises should always use the employee's actual working state, industry, establishment nature, number of employees and employee classification as the starting point for judgment. The amounts, thresholds and examples in this article are used to establish calculation methods and should not replace local legal advice for specific employees and establishments.

3. India Labor Law and Regulatory Framework

The core of India labor compliance is "central Codes plus local implementation." The four main Labor Codes include the Code on Wages, 2019; the Industrial Relations Code, 2020; the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (OSHWC Code); and the Code on Social Security, 2020.

Legal Framework Main Content Practical Focus
Code on WagesMinimum wage, wage payment, deductions, bonus and equal payMonthly wage payment deadline, termination wage deadline, 50% wage definition and deduction caps
Industrial Relations CodeTrade unions, grievances, standing orders, retrenchment and establishment closureWhether employee is a worker, headcount thresholds, notice, approval and compensation
OSHWC CodeWorking hours, safety, registration and contract laborDaily and weekly working hours, contractor arrangements, establishment registration and site responsibility
Code on Social SecurityEPF, ESI, gratuity, maternity leave and other securityCoverage thresholds, contribution base, fixed-term employee rights and employer registration
State Regulations and NotificationsShops and Establishments, minimum wage, holidays, professional tax and labor welfare fundMust be continuously updated according to the employee's actual working state

Enterprises can judge in the following order: first determine the actual work location and establishment nature, then determine whether the employee is a worker, manager, supervisor or fixed-term employee; then check establishment headcount, wages and prior enrollment status; finally combine central requirements with the state's Shops and Establishments Act, minimum wage and holiday notifications for implementation.

4. Recruitment, Offer and Onboarding

Common recruitment channels in India include the National Career Service (NCS), Naukri, LinkedIn, Foundit, Indeed India and iimjobs. Recruitment advertisements should accurately state the work location, employment entity, job responsibilities, contract type, wage structure and necessary qualifications, should not use discriminatory conditions, and should not package long-term controlled positions as independent contractor services.

Onboarding materials should be consistent with payslip items, avoiding the use of different names for the same item in the offer, contract and payroll system.

5. Employment Contracts, Contract Types and Probation Period

Common employment arrangements include permanent employment, fixed-term employment, project-based employment, third-party contract staffing and independent contracting. Direct fixed-term employees are not contract labor; under the Labor Codes, direct fixed-term employees may acquire gratuity eligibility after one year of continuous service.

Contract Type Applicable Scenario Key Compliance Point
Permanent EmploymentContinuous core positionsClearly state wages, duties, work location, leave, notice and confidentiality clauses
Fixed-term EmploymentDirect employment with a genuine term or project cycleState the term, early termination mechanism and settlement on expiry; must not be confused with third-party dispatch
Third-party Contract StaffingGenuine vendor services or dispatch arrangementsVerify contractor license, wages and social security, safety and principal employer backstop liability
Independent ContractingIndependent delivery of results, contractor arranges work autonomouslyCheck direction and control, fixed hours, exclusivity, tools and organizational integration to prevent misclassification

India does not have a unified statutory probation period applicable to all positions and states. Office positions commonly agree on 3 to 6 months, but judgment should still be combined with the contract, applicable state law, Standing Orders and the nature of the position. Probation does not mean dismissal without reason, evidence or procedure; enterprises should still record performance feedback, give an opportunity to explain, and pay wages and other amounts due as agreed.

6. Wages, Minimum Wage and Pre-tax Take-home Pay Calculation (Gross-to-Net)

India's minimum wage must be determined by the employee's actual working state, city, industry, establishment type, skill or education level and latest Variable Dearness Allowance (VDA) notification. For example, a Delhi operations specialist who is a university graduate with a monthly fixed wage of INR 24,000 would be below the above INR 24,356 standard. Employers cannot use performance bonuses or expense reimbursements to fill the fixed minimum wage gap.

From November 21, 2025, if excluded items in the wage definition exceed 50% of total remuneration, the excess portion may be re-included in wages. Employer contributions to provident fund or pension and statutory bonus participate in the 50% test, while gratuity, ESI and other retirement benefits generally do not participate in the test; annual performance incentives should also be judged by their specific nature.

Payslips should be provided at or before wage payment. Monthly wages usually should be paid by the 7th of the following month; wages payable after termination usually should be paid within 2 working days. Fines and deductions must have a lawful basis, and the total deductions in a single wage period generally must not exceed 50% of wages.

7. Working Hours, Overtime and Records

The general baseline for workers under the Labor Codes is 8 hours/day, 48 hours/week. The Delhi Shops and Establishments scenario usually has separate 9 hours/day, 48 hours/week; continuous work should not exceed 5 hours, after which at least a 30-minute rest should be arranged, and the total daily spread for commercial establishments usually does not exceed 10.5 hours.

Item Delhi Office Reference Employer Action
Normal Working Hours9 hours/day, 48 hours/week; Labor Code worker baseline 8 hours/daySet scheduling according to more specific, applicable or more favorable rules
RestAt least 30 minutes rest after maximum 5 hours continuous workRecord rest and actual working hours in the attendance system
OvertimeApplicable overtime exceeding 8 hours/day or 48 hours/week is usually paid at 2 times normal wageCalculate daily and weekly, do not replace actual overtime with fixed allowances
RecordsAttendance, scheduling, overtime, wage and deduction recordsKeep relevant wage records for at least 5 years

A job title containing "manager" does not automatically exclude working hours or overtime protection; the key is the employee's actual duties, decision-making authority and supervisory authority. Fixed overtime allowances can only serve as a prepayment mechanism; enterprises should still calculate based on actual working hours and make up the difference.

8. Statutory Holidays, Annual Leave and Other Statutory Leave

Delhi office employees usually enjoy at least 15 days of privilege leave after 12 months of continuous service; if less than one year, after 4 months of continuous work, 5 days of leave may be acquired for every 4 months completed, and after 1 month of continuous work, usually not less than 1 day of casual leave per month. The common carry-forward cap for privilege leave is 3 times the annual entitlement, i.e., 45 days; on departure, earned but untaken leave should be encashed according to applicable rules.

10. Local Employees vs Expatriates

Both local and foreign employees are subject to applicable labor regulations, contracts, wage payment and workplace policies, but foreign employees also involve visas, work authorization, tax residency, social security coverage and cross-border compensation. An employment contract itself cannot replace lawful work authorization.

Item Local Employees Expatriate or Foreign Employees
Identity and Work EligibilityVerify identity, PAN, bank and social security informationVerify Employment Visa, work activities, duration and registration requirements before entry
Social SecurityHandle according to applicable EPF, ESI, EDLI rulesFirst check international worker rules, social security agreements and home-country certificates; cannot directly apply local high-salary employee exemption logic
Payroll and TaxImplement according to Indian TDS and employee choicesSimultaneously check Indian tax residency, shadow payroll, cross-border benefits and tax treaties

Work permits and visa paths should be based on the Indian Ministry of Home Affairs, Indian embassies and consulates abroad and local immigration requirements. Enterprises should not allow foreign nationals to engage in actual employment work on a business visit status, nor should they pay Indian work remuneration only through an overseas payroll without checking tax and social security.

11. Remote Work, Data Privacy and Record Keeping

When a remote employee changes their actual working state, enterprises should re-check minimum wage, holidays, professional tax, labor welfare fund, establishment registration and social security coverage, rather than only modifying the HR system address. Cross-state remote arrangements may also change corporate tax presence and permanent establishment risk, requiring joint assessment by the tax team.

For workplace sexual harassment complaints, the official SHe-Box platform of the Indian Ministry of Women and Child Development can be referenced as a complaint entry point. Enterprises still need to fulfill Internal Committee, investigation procedure, confidentiality and anti-retaliation obligations.

12. Termination of Employment, Retrenchment Compensation and Final Settlement

India does not have a unified dismissal formula covering all employees and states. Enterprises must first determine whether the employee is a worker under the Industrial Relations Code, whether they hold managerial or supervisory duties, whether they are a direct fixed-term employee, whether they have completed one year of continuous service, and whether the establishment is a factory, mine or plantation and triggers the headcount threshold.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Does India have a national uniform minimum wage?
There is no single national amount that all enterprises can directly apply. Employers need to verify by actual working state, industry, establishment, skill or education level and latest Variable Dearness Allowance (VDA) notification.

Is the statutory cost for Indian employers simply 12% of wages?
No. 12% is usually only one of the common EPF contribution rates; employers may also bear EPS, EDLI, ESI, gratuity, statutory bonus, state-level items and scenario costs.

Can high-salary employees definitely be excluded from EPF?
Not necessarily. In addition to wage level, establishment coverage, whether the employee already has a UAN and prior EPF membership status must be checked. Existing membership continuity usually cannot be interrupted just because of a salary increase.

When is the latest monthly wage payment deadline in India?
According to the general rules of the Code on Wages, monthly wages usually should be paid by the 7th of the following month. Wages payable after termination usually should be paid within 2 working days; other settlement items are handled according to their respective statutory deadlines.

How many days of annual leave do Delhi office employees have per year?
Usually at least 15 days of privilege leave after 12 months of continuous service; carry-forward, early acquisition and departure encashment should also be combined with Delhi regulations, contract and more favorable enterprise policy.

Must all Indian private enterprises adopt the 17 holidays in this article?
No. This article lists the Delhi/New Delhi central government office 2026 reference calendar. Private enterprises need to confirm applicable paid holidays according to the actual working state, local holiday law, industry and contract.

Do fixed-term employees have gratuity?
Direct fixed-term employees may acquire gratuity eligibility after one year of continuous service under the Labor Codes; third-party contract labor and independent contracting arrangements need separate judgment based on the true relationship.

Can India labor risk be completely isolated through Employer of Record (EOR)?
No. Employer of Record services can support market entry and local employment within the permitted scope, but tax, permanent establishment, immigration, actual management, employee classification and termination responsibilities still need case-by-case confirmation.
