Minimum Wage, MPF, Statutory Holidays and Termination Compliance

2026 POLICY UPDATE

Continuous Contract Threshold Updated
From January 18, 2026, continuous contracts will use the "4-17 or 4-68" standard. Employers should update part-time and flexible scheduling tests and keep rolling four-week working hours.

Easter Monday Becomes a Statutory Holiday
From 2026, Easter Monday will be included as a statutory holiday. In 2026, the date is April 6. Employers should update holiday calendars, scheduling and holiday pay.

Minimum Wage and Record Threshold Increased
From May 1, 2026, the minimum wage will rise to HKD 43.1/hour, and the monthly salary threshold for working hours records will rise to HKD 17,600. Employers should switch payroll and attendance parameters.

Employment in China Hong Kong involves written employment terms, statutory minimum wage, continuous contracts, statutory holidays and annual leave, Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF), Employees' Compensation Insurance, tax filing, personal data protection and final settlement. When Chinese enterprises recruit employees in Hong Kong, they should first confirm the actual work location, employment entity, work rights, salary structure and working hours arrangements, and then configure MPF, leave and payroll parameters.

There are three changes in 2026 that require key implementation: from January 18, continuous contracts will use the "4-17 or 4-68" standard; Easter Monday will be added as a statutory holiday; from May 1, the statutory minimum wage will rise to HKD 43.1/hour, and the monthly salary threshold for working hours records will be adjusted to HKD 17,600.

This guide is applicable to Chinese enterprise HR, legal, finance and overseas business teams engaged in local employment, payroll calculation, Employer of Record (EOR) assessment, cross-border remote work or termination management in China Hong Kong. Visas and work permits are not covered in this article; work rights of non-local employees should be verified separately. The content was last verified on August 27, 2026.

1. 2026 China Hong Kong Employment Compliance Overview

One-sentence conclusion: Hong Kong employment rules are relatively clear, but you cannot just look at "low social security." The key parameters for 2026 are the minimum wage of HKD 43.1/hour, the monthly salary threshold for working hours records of HKD 17,600, MPF employer and employee contributions of 5% each with monthly mandatory contributions usually capped at HKD 1,500 each, and the difference between statutory holidays and public holidays.

The key to landing employment in Hong Kong is records. Payslips, contracts, attendance, leave, MPF contributions, tax filing and termination settlement should correspond with each other.

From January 18, 2026, continuous contracts adopt the "4-17 or 4-68" standard: the employee has been continuously employed by the same employer for 4 weeks and works at least 17 hours per week; if a week is less than 17 hours, the test is that week plus the immediately preceding three weeks totaling 68 hours. The employer bears the burden of proving that the employee is not under a continuous contract.

If the client directly manages the EOR employee's daily work, discipline and dismissal, it should also assess co-employment and actual employer risks.

Part-time, hourly and commission positions must keep actual working hours and wage composition. From January 18, 2026, it is no longer possible to directly exclude a continuous contract because a certain week is below 18 hours; the "4-17 or 4-68" test should be run.

5.1 Probation Period

Hong Kong law does not have a unified mandatory probation period length; it is usually agreed by contract. In practice, 1 to 3 months is common, and some positions may be longer, but the period should be reasonable and match the nature of the position.

6. Wages, Minimum Wage and Pre-tax Take-home Salary Calculation

6.1 Current Minimum Wage

Item Current Value Effective Date Execution Reminder
Statutory Minimum Wage HKD 43.1/hour May 1, 2026 Calculate average hourly wage based on accrued wages and total working hours in the wage period
Monthly Salary Threshold for Working Hours Records HKD 17,600/month May 1, 2026 When below this threshold, the employer needs to record total working hours in the wage period

Minimum wage comparison cannot simply use monthly salary divided by fixed hours. Enterprises need to calculate the average hourly wage based on accrued wages and total working hours in the wage period. Commission, allowances, bonuses, deductions, holiday pay and non-working time payments should be judged according to Labour Department guidelines as to whether they are included.

Assume a full-time operations specialist has a monthly salary of HKD 30,000 and works 5 days a week. This wage is usually clearly above the statutory minimum wage, but the employer still needs to pay wages on time, keep wage and leave records, make MPF contributions, manage statutory holidays and leave, and settle all amounts due on termination.

Minimum Wage Example: An hourly retail employee has 160 paid working hours in a wage period, all occurring after May 1, 2026. The minimum wage baseline is 160 × HKD 43.1 = HKD 6,896. If the accrued wages that can be included in the minimum wage comparison are only HKD 6,700, the employer needs to make up HKD 196.

When a wage period crosses May 1, 2026, working hours before April 30 should be calculated at the old rate, and working hours from May 1 at HKD 43.1 separately.

6.3 Wage Payment and Deductions

Wages should be paid as soon as possible after the last day of the wage period, usually no later than 7 days. On termination of employment, termination payments other than severance payment usually must also be paid no later than 7 days after termination.

Deductions can only be made within the scope permitted by law, such as absence, damage or loss of employer property subject to limits, advance wages or loan repayment. It is not permissible to withhold all wages on the grounds that equipment has not been returned or handover is incomplete.

Expense reimbursements should not be packaged as wages. Whether commission and recurring allowances are included in average wages, minimum wage or termination payments should be judged according to their nature. "All-inclusive salary" cannot eliminate statutory holiday pay, annual leave pay, payment in lieu of notice, severance payment or long service payment.

7. Working Hours, Overtime and Records

Hong Kong has no unified statutory maximum working hours, nor a generally applicable statutory overtime pay multiplier. Whether and how overtime is paid mainly depends on the employment contract, employee handbook and company policy.

Even if the contract does not provide for overtime pay, the average hourly wage in the wage period must still not be lower than the statutory minimum wage.

The employer may set a common leave year, for example January 1 to December 31 each year. When an employee joins during the common leave year and has less than 12 months, annual leave should be calculated pro-rata based on service period; less than 1 day is usually rounded up to 1 day.

Annual leave time is designated by the employer after consulting the employee, usually with at least 14 days' written notice. When annual leave coincides with rest days or statutory holidays, they should be handled separately, and the corresponding rights must not be directly absorbed.

Annual leave pay is usually calculated based on the average daily wage of the 12 months preceding the first day of annual leave; when employed less than 12 months, it is calculated based on the shorter employment period.

The employer may not require employees to give up statutory annual leave, but employees may choose to encash the portion exceeding 10 days.

11.2 Remote Work and Personal Data Protection

Employee personal data processing should comply with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance. Background checks, health data, performance monitoring, cross-border transfers, device monitoring and client system access should have lawful purposes, notice, minimization and retention periods.

Cross-border remote work requires separate assessment of tax, social security, visa, Employees' Compensation Insurance and permanent establishment risks at the actual work location. When an employee works long-term in the Mainland or a third place, a Hong Kong employment contract cannot automatically cover local labor law liabilities.

12. Termination of Employment, Compensation and Final Settlement

Ordinary dismissal cannot just look at "paying one month's notice." Enterprises should first determine whether the employee is under a continuous contract, whether they are in the first month of probation, the contractual notice period, the reason for termination and protection status, and then calculate payment in lieu of notice, unused annual leave, bonus, severance payment or long service payment.

12.5 Severance Payment, Long Service Payment and MPF Offset

Item Common Trigger Conditions Calculation Formula Operational Reminder
Severance Payment (SP) Continuous contract not less than 24 months, dismissed due to redundancy, fixed-term non-renewal due to redundancy or lay-off For monthly-rated employees, usually two-thirds × last month's wage × reckonable years of service Monthly wage calculation cap HKD 22,500, annual maximum HKD 15,000, total cap HKD 390,000

Severance Payment Example: Employee monthly salary HKD 30,000, 3 years of service and terminated due to position cancellation. Since the wage calculation cap is HKD 22,500, annual severance payment is HKD 15,000, totaling HKD 45,000 for 3 years.

Long Service Payment Example: Employee monthly salary HKD 20,000, 6 years of continuous service, ordinarily dismissed for non-redundancy reasons and meets long service payment conditions. Long service payment is 20,000 × two-thirds × 6 = HKD 80,000.

12.7 Prohibited or High-risk Dismissal Situations

Employers must not dismiss employees in specific protected situations, including when the employee has confirmed pregnancy and given notice, when the employee is on paid sick leave, when the employee provides evidence for enforcement of the Employment Ordinance or work injury and occupational safety matters, when the employee participates in trade union activities, and when a work-injured employee has not completed Employees' Compensation processing.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

What is the statutory minimum wage in China Hong Kong in 2026?
From May 1, 2026, the statutory minimum wage is HKD 43.1/hour. Wage periods crossing the effective date should calculate working hours before April 30 and from May 1 in segments.

What is the "4-17 or 4-68" continuous contract rule in 2026?
From January 18, 2026, an employee continuously employed by the same employer for 4 weeks and working at least 17 hours per week; if a week is less than 17 hours, that week plus the immediately preceding three weeks totaling not less than 68 hours may also constitute a continuous contract.

How much MPF do Hong Kong employers and employees each need to contribute?
Usually both parties contribute 5% of relevant income each, with a monthly relevant income cap of HKD 30,000, so both parties' monthly mandatory contributions are usually capped at HKD 1,500 each. Low-income employees and statutory exemptions need separate judgment.

How many statutory holidays are there in Hong Kong in 2026?
There are 15 statutory holidays in 2026, among which Easter Monday has been included as a statutory holiday from 2026. Public holidays are broader in scope; enterprises should judge whether all apply according to contract and company policy.

Does an employee working less than 12 months have no annual leave rights at all?
It cannot be generalized. While still employed, the employee usually has not yet reached the point of acquiring ordinary statutory annual leave after completing the first 12 months, but when employment terminates after 3 months of service and it is not summary dismissal, pro-rata annual leave pay usually needs to be settled; a common leave year may also produce pro-rata arrangements.

How is severance payment calculated in Hong Kong redundancy?
Eligible monthly-rated employees are usually calculated as two-thirds of the last month's wage multiplied by reckonable years of service, with a monthly wage calculation cap of HKD 22,500 and a total cap of HKD 390,000. The MPF offset transition rules should also be handled according to service before and after May 1, 2025.

Can EOR automatically solve Hong Kong work visas and MPF exemptions?
No. Employer of Record (EOR) cannot replace work visas, nor can it automatically confirm MPF exemption, tax residency or cross-border remote work arrangements.
