Minimum Wage, Employment Contracts, BPJS, THR and Termination Compliance

2026 POLICY UPDATE

Jakarta Minimum Wage Adjusted
From January 1, 2026, the DKI Jakarta UMP is adjusted to IDR 5,729,876 per month. Employers should update Jakarta recruitment standards, in-service wage structures and annual cost budgets.

New Outsourcing Rules Implemented
Ministry of Manpower Regulation No. 7 of 2026 has been in effect since April 30, 2026. Enterprises should verify the scope of outsourced positions, supplier legal personality, written agreements and the 3-working-day registration requirement.

Annual Holiday Arrangement Updated
The official 2026 calendar confirms 17 national holidays and 8 collective leave days. Private enterprises should separately configure national holidays, annual leave deductions, collective leave and continuous operation scheduling rules.

Employing staff in Indonesia cannot be done by only calculating wages and social security ratios. The complete employer cost may also include religious holiday allowance (Tunjangan Hari Raya, THR), overtime pay, fixed-term employment contract compensation, unused annual leave and termination compensation. The minimum wage is determined separately according to the employee's actual working province, city or regency; the Jakarta standard cannot be directly applied nationwide.

This guide is intended for Chinese enterprise HR, legal, finance and overseas business teams, covering recruitment, employment contracts, payroll calculation (Payroll), social security, leave, foreign employees, termination of employment and Employer of Record (EOR) and other main modules. Matters involving foreign employee work permits, special industries, collective agreements or labor disputes still require case-by-case confirmation with local professional advice.

3. Indonesia Labor Law and Regulatory Framework

Indonesia labor relations are mainly governed by the Labor Law, the Job Creation Law and its implementing regulations, local minimum wage decisions and Ministry of Manpower regulations. Main regulatory bodies include the Indonesian Ministry of Manpower, local labor authorities, BPJS Employment Security Agency, BPJS Health Security Agency and local governments.

PKWTT may agree on a probation period, but usually no more than 3 months, and wages during probation must still not be lower than the applicable minimum wage. PKWT usually must not have a probation period; writing a probation clause into PKWT may render the clause invalid or cause a labor dispute.

Extension or termination of probation should be handled based on written contract, objective assessment and lawful procedure. Enterprises cannot use probation to circumvent discrimination, retaliation, pregnancy or work injury protection, nor withhold wages and statutory rights the employee has already earned.

6. Wages, Minimum Wage and Pre-tax Take-home Pay Calculation (Gross-to-Net)

Minimum wage should be determined by the employee's actual main work location. Enterprises need to compare the provincial minimum wage (UMP), city/regency minimum wage (UMK) and possibly applicable industry minimum wage, and adopt the applicable higher standard. Minimum wage usually focuses on protecting employees with less than one year of service; for employees with one year or more of service, enterprises should still establish wage structure and grades and negotiate adjustments based on capability, seniority and performance.

For example, a Jakarta office operations specialist with a monthly wage of IDR 12,000,000 is above the 2026 Jakarta UMP. But if the employee works remotely long-term from another city, the enterprise must not continue to judge only by the Jakarta standard; it should re-check the UMP, UMK and industry requirements of the employee's actual work location.

Non-fixed meal, transport allowances, expense reimbursements or overtime pay should not be arbitrarily used to fill the minimum wage gap. The payslip should separately list basic wage, fixed and non-fixed allowances, overtime pay, THR, BPJS employee share and personal income tax (PPh 21). Wage reduction, deferred payment or payment in kind requires separate legal basis and employee documentation.

Religious Holiday or RamadanEnterprise may lawfully adjust working hours or schedulingCoordinate THR, leave and business continuity in advance

Overtime hourly wage is usually calculated based on monthly wage divided by 173. The multipliers for the first hour on a workday, subsequent hours, weekly rest days and statutory holidays differ, and different calculation steps may apply under five-day and six-day systems, so "monthly salary already includes all overtime" cannot replace true records and calculation.

Whether managers are excluded from overtime should be judged based on actual duties, decision-making authority and company regulations; using only the "manager" job title is not sufficient. Before monthly closing, employees and supervisors should confirm actual working hours, overtime authorization and corresponding dates.

9. Employer Social Security, Mandatory Benefits and Taxation

Employers must register eligible employees with BPJS Employment Security and BPJS Health Security, determine the wage base and work injury security (JKK) risk class, withhold the employee share from wages and pay it together with the employer share. THR is not a social security rate but is a mandatory annual cash obligation that must be accrued separately.

Both local and foreign employees are subject to applicable employment contracts, wages, working hours, leave and termination requirements. Foreign employees also involve foreign worker utilization plan, visa, residence, work authorization, tax and social security applicability. Signing an employment contract does not mean lawful work eligibility has been obtained.

12. Termination of Employment, Severance Compensation and Final Settlement

Termination initiated by the employer outside probation in Indonesia must usually be based on a true reason recognized by current regulations, and different procedures and compensation multipliers apply based on the reason. Paying severance or giving notice cannot replace statutory reason. Enterprises should separately record the notice delivery date, employee objection period, bilateral negotiation date, last actual working day and legal termination date of the employment relationship.

PKWTT severance base increases with tenure: less than 1 year is 1 month's wage, 1 to less than 2 years is 2 months, then usually increases by 1 month for each additional year, up to a maximum of 9 months for 8 years or more. Tenure award starts from 3 years of service: 3 to less than 6 years is 2 months' wage, 6 to less than 9 years is 3 months, then increases per rules, and 24 years or more is 10 months.

Rights compensation usually includes qualifying unused annual leave, employee and family return travel costs to the place of employment, and other items stipulated in contract, company regulations or collective agreement. The actual amount equals the statutory base multiplied by the termination reason multiplier, plus rights compensation. Government Regulation No. 35/2021 provides different combinations for efficiency, merger, closure, bankruptcy, retirement, long-term illness and misconduct, so the final amount must not be promised before the true legal reason is determined.

PKWT employees with at least 1 month of continuous service usually enjoy compensation at contract expiry or applicable end point. 12 months of service is 1 month's wage; less or more than 12 months is calculated as "months of service ÷ 12 × 1 month's wage." On renewal, the previous contract period compensation is usually settled first, then re-accrued for the renewal.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Does Indonesia have a national uniform minimum wage?
No. Minimum wage is usually determined by the province, city or regency where the employee actually works; enterprises need to compare applicable UMP, UMK and industry standards. The 2026 Jakarta UMP cannot be directly applied to other regions.

What is the Jakarta minimum wage in 2026?
The DKI Jakarta 2026 provincial minimum wage is IDR 5,729,876 per month, effective from January 1, 2026. Before formal payroll, the position should still be checked for whether a higher local or industry standard applies.

Is the statutory cost for Indonesian employers simply BPJS?
No. In addition to BPJS Employment and Health Security, employers also need to consider THR, overtime, annual leave, PKWT compensation, PKWTT termination costs, tax operations and service costs.

Can PKWT have a probation period?
Usually no. Probation generally applies only to PKWTT and is usually maximum 3 months. A probation clause in PKWT may be invalid and cause disputes.

When should THR be paid?
THR usually must be paid at least 7 days before the employee's religious holiday. 12 months of service is usually 1 month's wage; 1 month but less than 12 months is pro-rated by months of service.

Are all 8 collective leave days in 2026 additional paid leave for private enterprises?
No. Whether private enterprises implement collective leave is usually decided by the enterprise head; once implemented it generally deducts from the employee's annual leave balance and cannot automatically be regarded as 8 additional statutory paid holidays.

Does PKWT contract expiry require compensation?
Usually yes. PKWT employees with at least 1 month of continuous service usually enjoy compensation calculated by service period at contract expiry or applicable end point; early termination may also involve remaining term wage liability.

Can using Employer of Record (EOR) eliminate all risks?
No. EOR can support local employment within the permitted scope, but tax, permanent establishment, foreign employees, actual management, position classification and termination responsibilities still need case-by-case confirmation.
