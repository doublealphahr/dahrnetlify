Minimum Wage, Modern Awards, Super, Leave, Termination, and EOR Compliance

2026 POLICY UPDATE

Minimum wage and Modern Awards raised from July 1: National minimum wage rises to AUD 1,004.90/week or AUD 26.44/hour, and modern award minimum wages generally increase 4.75%; employers should update award classification, payroll, and overtime allowance parameters.

Payday Super effective from July 1: Employers generally must contribute 12% of qualifying earnings in superannuation with each pay run, and ensure payment reaches the employee fund within 7 business days. Companies should stop using the old quarterly contribution process.

Fair Work Commission procedure updated from July 31: MyFWC electronic filing, enterprise agreement original digital files, and some dispute forms and service procedures changed. Employers should keep receipts and timestamps and use the latest procedural documents.

Australia's employment compliance is composed of the Fair Work Act, National Employment Standards (NES), Modern Awards, enterprise agreements, superannuation, PAYG withholding, state payroll tax, workers' compensation, and occupational health and safety rules. When Chinese companies recruit in Australia or use Employer of Record (EOR) services, they cannot quote only by national minimum wage or "wage plus 12% super"; they also need to verify position classification, work state, overtime and allowances, leave, insurance, and potential termination costs.

From July 1, 2026, the national minimum wage is adjusted to AUD 1,004.90/week or AUD 26.44/hour, and modern award minimum wages generally increase 4.75%; Payday Super takes effect simultaneously, requiring employers to generally contribute 12% of qualifying earnings with wages and ensure payment reaches the employee fund within 7 business days. Companies should update offers, payroll systems, award classification, super payment, and quotation processes.

2. Three Rules to Watch Most in 2026

National minimum wage and modern award wages raised from July 1

From the first full wage cycle starting on or after July 1, 2026, national minimum wage is AUD 1,004.90/week or AUD 26.44/hour, based on 38-hour workweek. Casual Employee applying national minimum wage, after including 25% casual loading, minimum is AUD 33.05/hour. Modern award minimum wages generally increase 4.75%. Employers must first confirm applicable industry, position level, allowances, overtime, and penalty rates, and cannot quote only by national minimum wage.

Payday Super formally implemented

From July 1, 2026, employers generally must calculate and pay superannuation at 12% of employee Qualifying Earnings with each pay run, and payment generally must reach employee fund within 7 business days from payday. Companies should upgrade Payroll and STP codes, handle first contribution, returned amounts, fund verification, and error correction, and stop using the old quarterly contribution process.

Fair Work Commission electronic procedure updated

From July 31, 2026, Fair Work Commission's new application, electronic filing, and service rules take effect. After submitting through MyFWC, keep confirmation notice, submission receipt, and timestamp; when applying for approval of enterprise agreement, also keep and submit original digital format files. When involving regulated platform workers, labor dispatch, and right to disconnect disputes, use the latest forms and procedural time limits.

10. Local Employees and Expatriate or Foreign Employees

Foreign employees must hold work rights consistent with position, statutory employer, and actual work arrangement before starting work. An employment contract or EOR arrangement itself cannot generate a visa, nor prove that the service provider automatically has qualification to sponsor any visa category.

Foreign employees actually working in Australia are generally also protected by National Employment Standards, Modern Awards, minimum wage, superannuation, PAYG, workers' compensation, occupational health and safety, and fair dismissal rules. Employers must not lower statutory entitlements or exclude statutory rights because employee holds a temporary visa.

Expatriate employees also need to verify tax resident status, overseas wages, housing and vehicle benefits, shadow payroll, double taxation, permanent establishment, and cross-state work arrangements. When employee changes work state, position, client, or remote location, award classification, public holidays, payroll tax, workers' compensation, and visa conditions should be re-checked.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the 2026 Australia national minimum wage?
From July 1, 2026, the national minimum wage is AUD 1,004.90/week or AUD 26.44/hour, based on 38-hour workweek. This standard mainly applies to national workplace relations system employees not covered by Modern Award or enterprise agreement; most positions still need to verify award and classification first.

Can all employees be directly quoted at AUD 26.44/hour?
No. Employees may be affected by modern award, enterprise agreement, casual loading, junior rates, trainee or apprentice rates, overtime, night shift, weekend, and public holiday rates, and actual minimum entitlement may be higher than national minimum wage.

What is the Australian employer superannuation rate?
The 2026 statutory Superannuation Guarantee rate is generally 12% of qualifying earnings. After Payday Super takes effect from July 1, employers generally should pay with each pay run and ensure payment reaches employee fund within 7 business days.

How many hours per week does a full-time Australian employee work?
Under National Employment Standards, full-time employee ordinary working hours are generally 38 hours/week, plus reasonable additional hours. Reasonableness needs to be judged considering health and safety, family responsibility, position nature, advance notice, and compensation.

What is the statutory annual leave in Australia?
Full-time and part-time employees generally 4 weeks annual leave per year; qualifying shiftworker generally 5 weeks. Annual leave accrues continuously by ordinary hours from day one, and unused balance already formed when departing before one year generally must still be paid.

Can an employee be directly dismissed during probation?
Cannot be simply understood as risk-free dismissal. Probation does not exclude minimum wage, super, notice period, anti-discrimination, and general protections. Even if employee has not reached unfair dismissal minimum service period, employer should still verify genuine reason, contract, award, and protected rights.

How is Australian redundancy pay calculated?
When meeting National Employment Standards and not applying small business or other exception, statutory redundancy pay is generally calculated based on continuous service years, commonly 4 to 16 weeks, but service of 10 years or more generally falls back to 12 weeks. Notice pay, unused annual leave, and other amounts still need separate settlement.

Can a company hire employees without an Australian entity?
Companies may evaluate a compliant Employer of Record (EOR), but must confirm case by case the statutory employer, award classification, PAYG, STP, superannuation, workers' compensation, work rights, client management boundary, and termination responsibility.
