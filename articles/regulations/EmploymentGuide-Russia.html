Employment Contracts, Wage Taxes, Leave, Termination and EOR Compliance

2026 POLICY UPDATE

Federal Minimum Wage: From January 1, 2026, the federal MROT is RUB 27,093/month; regional, industry or northern standards may be higher.

Unified Insurance Contribution Ceiling: The 2026 annual cumulative ceiling per employee is RUB 2,979,000; the general rate is 30% within the ceiling and 15.1% on the excess.

Minimum Base for Heads of Commercial Organizations: The monthly unified insurance contribution base for a head generally must not be lower than one federal MROT; low-paid, unpaid or dormant entities also need to check.

Some SME Preferences Tightened: Preferential rates no longer depend only on SME status, but also require checking the main activity list, registration, revenue share and wage range; failure to meet conditions may result in loss of the preference from the beginning of the period.

Hiring employees in Russia means companies cannot simply convert RMB salaries into rubles and pay directly. Employment contracts, HR orders, wage payments, personal income tax (NDFL), social insurance, work accident insurance, working time, leave and separation documents all need to be borne by a compliant local employer and kept consistent with the actual management approach.

This guide is intended for Chinese companies planning to recruit in Russia, sign contracts, configure payroll, calculate employer costs, or compare a local entity, Employer of Record (EOR) and payroll outsourcing. All amounts in this document are expressed in Russian rubles (RUB); where regional minimum wages, northern coefficients, industry agreements, foreign status, sanctions, payment channels or cross-border data are involved, a special verification should be conducted before execution.

2. Three Rules Most in Need of Update in 2026

01 | Federal minimum wage increased to RUB 27,093/month. This amount is the federal baseline for completing a full month of normal working time and does not automatically replace a higher regional minimum wage, industry agreement, collective agreement or applicable regional coefficient and northern allowance. Quotation systems, contract templates and minimum wage warning lines should be updated simultaneously.

02 | Unified insurance contribution annual ceiling raised to RUB 2,979,000 per person. General contributors apply 30% to payments within the ceiling and 15.1% to the excess. This ceiling accumulates per employee, per calendar year, not by total company payroll. When changing payroll service providers or systems, the same employee's historical tax base for the current year must be migrated.

03 | Minimum contribution base requirement for heads of commercial organizations. From 2026, even if the monthly remuneration of the head of a commercial organization is below the federal minimum wage, or even if no salary is actually calculated, the unified insurance contribution may need to be calculated on at least one federal minimum wage base. Shell companies, dormant entities, multi-head arrangements and situations where the head serves at multiple companies should all be reviewed separately.

3. Russian Labor Law and Regulatory Framework

The core basis of the Russian employment relationship is the Labor Code of the Russian Federation. The Labor Code covers employment contracts, wages, working time, leave, occupational safety, discipline, changes in labor relations and termination. Tax, social insurance, personal data, immigration and industry norms are supplemented by relevant laws and competent authority rules respectively.

RoskomnadzorPersonal data and communications supervisionData localization, cross-border transfer, data security and notification assessment

The local contracting employer is generally responsible for signing employment contracts, issuing HR orders, paying wages, withholding NDFL, paying insurance, keeping labor records, implementing occupational health and safety (HSE), managing leave and lawfully terminating employment. Instructions from an overseas parent company or business client cannot replace the statutory decisions and documents that the local employer must make.

4. Recruitment, Job Offer and Onboarding

Recruitment advertisements should not set gender, age, nationality, marital status or other restrictions unrelated to the position. Where there are genuine statutory right to work, qualification, health or safety requirements, they should be accurately described based on job needs and legal basis.

The offer should clarify whether it is binding and be consistent with the formal employment contract. It is recommended to at least state the position, work city, gross ruble wage, bonus logic, work model, proposed start date, contract term and preconditions. Do not promise a "fixed net salary" in the offer while passing all exchange rate, personal income tax or bank fees on to the employee.

If Chinese headquarters needs to access Russian employee data, data localization, cross-border transfer and security assessments should be completed before opening HRIS permissions.

5. Employment Contracts, Contract Types and Probationary Period

Ongoing positions generally use open-ended employment contracts. Fixed-term contracts should only be used in temporary, replacement, seasonal or project scenarios permitted by the Labor Code, generally not exceeding 5 years; lack of lawful reason or continued work after expiry may be deemed an open-ended contract.

Contract Type Typical Scenario Main Risk
Open-endedThe usual choice for ongoing positionsEmployer can only terminate based on statutory grounds and procedure
Fixed-termReplacing an absent employee, seasonal or legally permitted temporary workInsufficient reason, repeated renewal or failure to handle expiry may convert to open-ended
Part-time/concurrentLess than normal working hours or employee has another employerWages may be calculated by hours, but minimum wage test still required
Remote/hybridFully or partially remote workMust specify equipment, costs, communication, electronic documents and recall mechanism
Temporary or seasonalSpecific short work not exceeding 2 months or objective seasonal workSpecial rules for probation, notice and leave calculation
Civil law service contractGenuinely independent service providerIf subordinate management exists, may be reclassified as an employment relationship

The employment contract should at least state the contract parties, job duties, work location, start date, contract term and reason, wage structure, two pay days, working hours and rest, probationary period, working conditions and statutory guarantees, consistent with the actual arrangement. Remote, concurrent, shift, northern region or hazardous positions should include corresponding clauses, and cannot simply change the template title.

The probationary period for ordinary employees is generally up to 3 months; specific management positions such as company head, deputy head, chief accountant and branch head may be up to 6 months; for fixed-term contracts of 2–6 months, probation is generally at most 2 weeks. Pregnant women, minors and other categories listed by law may not be subject to a probationary period.

If the employer terminates the contract for failed probation, it generally needs to give at least 3 days' written notice and specifically explain the failure facts. "Not fitting the team" or client dissatisfaction alone is generally not sufficient. Companies should retain job descriptions, goals, feedback, work results and delivery evidence. Employees resigning during probation generally also apply 3 days' written notice; continued work after the probationary period generally means probation has been passed.

6. Wages, Minimum Wage and Gross-to-Net Calculation

From January 1, 2026, the federal minimum wage is RUB 27,093/month. Employees who complete a full month of normal working time must not be paid below the applicable floor. Regional minimum wages, industry agreements, collective agreements and regional/northern coefficients may create higher standards; part-time or incomplete months may be calculated pro-rata by actual working hours, but attendance and wage composition basis must be retained.

Wages are generally paid at least every half month. Specific pay days should be written into internal labor rules, collective agreement or employment contract, and generally must not be later than 15 calendar days after the end of the corresponding wage calculation period. Wages are paid in rubles, and the payslip should separately show basic wage, bonus, overtime, holiday pay, deductions and net wage.

Moscow Office Employee Example

Assume the employee's monthly gross wage is RUB 200,000, with no additional deductions, and annual main wage income is exactly RUB 2,400,000:

Item Calculation Amount
Monthly gross wageFixedRUB 200,000
Annual gross wage200,000 × 12RUB 2,400,000
Annual NDFL example2,400,000 × 13%RUB 312,000
Annual net example2,400,000 − 312,000RUB 2,088,000
Average monthly net example2,088,000 ÷ 12RUB 174,000

This example does not consider tax deductions, non-wage income, tax status changes, bonuses and other special tax bases. RUB 200,000 is only a demonstration wage, not a uniform statutory wage in Moscow. If cumulative main income crosses a tax bracket, only the portion exceeding the corresponding threshold applies the higher rate.

Companies must not use bonuses, northern coefficients, overtime or holiday pay to mask a non-compliant basic wage structure, nor arbitrarily deduct fines from wages. Frequent, fixed and unclear bonuses may also create enforceable employee rights.

7. Working Time, Overtime and Records

Normal working time in Russia generally does not exceed 40 hours per week. Employers should keep per-employee attendance, shifts, night work, overtime, weekend and statutory holiday work records, and cannot rely on verbal manager confirmation or only reconstruct working hours at payroll settlement.

Remote employees also need auditable working time and offline rules. A fixed monthly salary cannot automatically absorb all overtime; the legal basis, hours and compensation for overtime, night work and holiday work should be documented separately.

8. Statutory Holidays, Annual Leave and Other Statutory Leave

Employees generally enjoy 28 calendar days of basic paid annual leave per working year. In the first year, the right to use annual leave is generally acquired after 6 months of work, but pregnant women, minors and other statutory categories may take leave earlier in accordance with law. Annual leave may be divided, but generally at least one segment must not be less than 14 calendar days.

Scenario Basic Rule HR/Payroll Action
Leave scheduleEmployer should establish annual leave arrangements in advanceNotify employee and track changes
Statutory holiday falling within annual leaveNon-working statutory holidays generally not counted as annual leave daysSystem automatically extends
Leave carryoverShould be lawfully transferred and used within applicable periodDo not accumulate long-term or automatically reset to zero
In-service cash substitutionCash substitution of basic annual leave is restrictedHandle only within legally permitted scope
SeparationAll lawful unused annual leave should be compensated in cashInclude in final settlement on termination date

For separation before a full working year, unused leave is generally pro-rated based on months of service and other factors. Simplified example: an employee works 6 months, estimated at 28 days basic annual leave as 28 ÷ 12 × 6 = 14 days; the actual result should still be reviewed according to full service months, days already taken and the statutory average wage formula.

Working days before statutory holidays are generally shortened by 1 hour. Sick leave is generally managed through electronic sick leave certificates, with the employer generally bearing the first 3 days and SFR paying thereafter according to rules. Maternity leave, parental leave, study leave and unpaid leave apply separate eligibility, duration, job protection and payment rules, and should not all be treated as ordinary annual leave.

9. Employer Social Security, Mandatory Benefits and Tax

The unified insurance contribution and work accident insurance for ordinary employees are mainly employer costs borne outside gross wages; NDFL is borne by the employee and withheld by the employer as tax agent. A "30% social security" cannot be further deducted from employee wages, and work accident insurance should not be merged into the unified insurance rate.

Item Employer Responsibility/Cost Employee Responsibility/Deduction 2026 Parameter
Unified insurance contributionGenerally 30% within tax base, 15.1% on excess over annual ceilingNo corresponding deduction for ordinary employeesAnnual ceiling RUB 2,979,000 per employee
Work accident insurancePaid according to main activity and occupational risk classNone32 classes, 0.2%–8.5%
NDFLCalculate, withhold, report and payProgressive 13%–22%Determined by income type, cumulative tax base and status
Sick leave benefitGenerally bears the first 3 daysNo separate employee contributionSubsequent generally paid by SFR according to rules
Supplementary medical/pensionBorne only when committed by contract or company planMay have voluntary sharingNot a uniform statutory employee deduction

The progressive rates for main wage income are generally: 13% for the portion of annual tax base not exceeding RUB 2.4m; 15% for the portion exceeding RUB 2.4m to 5m; 18% for the portion exceeding 5m to 20m; 20% for the portion exceeding 20m to 50m; 22% for the portion exceeding 50m. Payroll should be calculated based on the employee's cumulative tax base at the same tax agent during the year; residents, non-residents, EAEU employees and special income still need separate verification.

Taking a monthly salary of RUB 200,000, a low-risk office work accident rate of 0.2%, and the employee's cumulative annual total not yet exceeding the insurance ceiling as an example:

Employer-Side Item Calculation Monthly Amount
Gross wageFixedRUB 200,000
Unified insurance contribution200,000 × 30%RUB 60,000
Work accident insurance200,000 × 0.2%RUB 400
Example employer cost subtotalTotal aboveRUB 260,400

After the employee's annual cumulative total exceeds RUB 2,979,000, the general unified insurance rate switches to 15.1%, and 30% cannot be used fixed throughout the year. Preferential rates may only be adopted when the entity, main activity, registration, revenue share and wage range conditions are all met; the "SME" or "IT company" label alone is not sufficient.

10. Local Employees and Posted/Foreign Employees

Local Russian employees are generally handled under general labor, NDFL and social insurance rules. Foreign or posted employees, in addition to labor law, also involve entry, residence, work permit or patent, highly qualified specialist status, immigration notification and tax status. Signing an employment contract does not mean the employee has already obtained the legal right to work.

Verification Dimension Local Employees Posted/Foreign Employees
Right to workGenerally no additional permit requiredConfirm permit or exemption based on nationality, status and position
NDFLGenerally handled under general cumulative ratesNeed to verify tax residency, EAEU or other special rules
Social insuranceGenerally applies general rulesConfirm item by item based on status, treaty and insurance coverage
Contract and notificationGeneral employment contract and HR registrationMay need to include permit information and fulfill immigration notification
Wage paymentRuble payment and local wage rulesAlso check bank, sanctions and cross-border payment paths
TerminationAccording to general statutory grounds and procedureAlso assess permit invalidation and immigration notification consequences

Chinese companies should set labor compliance and immigration compliance as two mutually verifying processes: HR is responsible for contracts, wages and HR documents, immigration consultants are responsible for status and notification, and payroll is responsible for tax status and tax/social security calculation. If either process is incomplete, the employee should not be arranged to actually start work.

11. Remote Work, Data Privacy and Record Keeping

Remote or hybrid employment contracts/supplementary agreements should specify the work location or location selection mechanism, communication method, working hours, equipment provision, expense reimbursement, electronic signing, deliverable submission, information security and return-to-office arrangements. Remote work does not automatically exempt Russian wage, working time, leave and occupational safety obligations.

Employee passports, wages, health, discipline and right to work materials are high-risk information. Russian personal data rules may require localization using databases located in Russia, and may set notification, assessment or other requirements for cross-border transfer. Before overseas cloud HRIS, Chinese headquarters access and global shared service centers process data, a data flow mapping and Roskomnadzor compliance assessment should be completed.

12. Termination of Employment, Severance Pay and Offboarding Settlement

Russia does not have a general mechanism for "paying a sum of compensation to dismiss without reason." Employer dismissal must fall within statutory grounds and complete fact investigation, employee explanation, notice, union or employment agency procedures, job offer and written order matching that reason. Employee resignation, negotiated termination, fixed-term expiry, failed probation, disciplinary dismissal and redundancy are different paths and cannot be mixed.

Scenario Basic Procedure Main Settlement
Employer termination during probationGenerally at least 3 days' written notice and statement of failure evidenceFinal pay, unused leave compensation and due expenses
Employee resignationGenerally 3 days during probation; generally at least 2 weeks outside probation, with exceptions for specific positionsFinal pay and leave compensation settled on termination date
Fixed-term expiryGenerally at least 3 calendar days' notice, with exceptions such as replacement contractsFinal pay and unused leave compensation
Disciplinary or performance-related dismissalOnly statutory grounds; complete explanation, evidence, deadlines and proportionality reviewImproper dismissal may lead to reinstatement, back pay and compensation
RedundancyGenuine position reduction, generally at least 2 months' notice, and fulfill job offer, employment agency and union obligationsSeverance and average wage guarantee during job search period
Negotiated terminationBoth parties genuinely, voluntarily and in writing agreeAmount, tax, payment date and document delivery clearly stated

Redundancy generally pays 1 month's average monthly wage as severance on the separation date; the second month during job search may continue to retain the average wage (less severance already paid), and under specific conditions the third month may be decided by the employment agency. Far North regions, collective agreements or employment contracts may provide better protection. If the employee agrees in writing to leave before the 2-month notice period expires, generally an additional average wage compensation for the unexpired period is also required.

Severance1 × 200,000RUB 200,000.00

The actual average wage must be recalculated according to the statutory reference period, bonuses already calculated and excluded periods, and the second or third month average wage guarantee is not automatically paid in a lump sum to all employees. On the termination date, final pay, unused leave, severance and other due amounts should be completed, and the labor record, certificates and order copies delivered; the termination date must be consistent in HR, payroll and reporting systems.

13. Choosing an Employment Model: Entity, Employer of Record (EOR) and Payroll Outsourcing

Model Suitable Scenario Company Retained Responsibility Key Risk

When assessing an EOR or similar arrangement, confirm the contracting employer, supplier qualifications, actual employee management, wage payment channel, FTS/SFR reporting, personal data localization, HSE and foreign right to work case by case. The end of a client project does not mean the employment contract automatically terminates, and banking or sanctions difficulties do not automatically exempt the obligation to pay wages on time and settle separation.

When choosing a model, it is recommended to compare the full lifecycle cost, not just the service fee: fixed wages, employer insurance, employee withholding, annual leave and sick leave, overtime and holidays, recruitment, equipment, termination reserve, legal support and payment failure contingency should all be included.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

What is the 2026 Russian minimum wage?
From January 1, 2026, the federal MROT is RUB 27,093/month. Regional, industry or northern standards may be higher, and companies should verify based on the employee's actual work location.

Is the unified insurance contribution 30% for the whole year?
No. The general rate is 30% within the annual cumulative base of RUB 2,979,000 per employee, and 15.1% on the excess. Payroll should be calculated based on each employee's cumulative value during the year.

Do heads of commercial organizations have a minimum contribution base?
Yes. From 2026, even if the head's monthly remuneration is below the federal minimum wage or no salary is actually calculated, the unified insurance contribution may need to be calculated on at least one federal minimum wage base.

How many days of annual leave do Russian employees have per year?
The basic paid annual leave is generally 28 calendar days per working year. In the first year, the right to use leave is generally acquired after 6 months of work, but statutory categories such as pregnant women and minors may take leave earlier.

Can an employer dismiss an employee during probation?
Yes, but generally at least 3 days' written notice is required, and the failure facts must be specifically explained. "Not fitting the team" or client dissatisfaction alone is generally not sufficient; job description, goals, feedback, work results and delivery evidence should be retained.

Does Russia have a uniform statutory severance pay?
There is no general mechanism for dismissing without reason by paying compensation. Redundancy generally pays 1 month's average monthly wage as severance, and the second or third month during job search may continue to retain the average wage under specific conditions.

Can employees be hired without a Russian entity?
Companies may assess a compliant EOR or similar arrangement, but must confirm the local contracting employer, supplier qualifications, actual management, wage payment channel, FTS/SFR reporting, personal data localization, HSE and foreign right to work case by case.

Do remote employees still need working time records?
Yes. Remote work does not automatically exempt Russian wage, working time, leave and occupational safety obligations, and the remote location, equipment, costs, communication and information security should also be agreed in writing.
