2. Three Rules to Watch Most in 2026

Mainland Minimum Wage Increased to EUR 920

From January 1, 2026, the Portuguese mainland minimum monthly wage increased from EUR 870 to EUR 920. Employers should simultaneously update the 14-month annual budget, part-time wage ratios, overtime hourly rates, social security bases and related allowance parameters.

Autonomous Region Minimum Wages Must Be Verified Separately

The Azores 2026 regional minimum wage is EUR 966/month, and Madeira also determines its 2026 standard separately through regional legislation. Companies must use the corresponding amount based on the employee's actual work region and cannot cover autonomous region employees with the mainland EUR 920.

2026 Personal Income Tax Withholding Tables Updated

The Portuguese Tax and Customs Authority has published the 2026 personal income tax (IRS) withholding tables for mainland employment income. Employers should update payroll systems based on employee income, family status and applicable region, and cannot promise net pay using a uniform income tax rate.

3. Portuguese Labor Law and Regulatory Framework

Portuguese labor relations are mainly governed by the Labor Code, collective labor regulation instruments and collective agreements, social security, tax, occupational safety, equal treatment and data protection rules. The Authority for Working Conditions (ACT) is responsible for labor conditions supervision, the Social Security (Segurança Social) administers social insurance, and the Tax and Customs Authority handles personal income tax withholding and reporting.

The contracting employer bears responsibility for signing contracts, onboarding registration, payroll, employee withholding, employer contributions, work accident insurance, occupational health and safety, working time and leave, discipline and termination. Client business managers may provide position, scheduling and performance facts, but must not bypass the contracting employer to directly change wages, discipline or dismiss EOR employees.

Companies should first confirm the actual work region and contracting employer, then identify the position, employee category and applicable collective agreement, and subsequently lock in the 14-month wage structure, working time, tax and social security, insurance and leave parameters. When position, region, working hours or remote arrangements change, a compliance reassessment should be conducted.

4. Recruitment, Job Offer and Onboarding

Job descriptions should specify actual duties, work location, reporting line, working hours or shifts, travel, language and objective qualifications, and must not collect photos, marital or parental status, health, religion or other protected information unrelated to the position.

The job offer should separately list basic gross salary, holiday allowance, Christmas allowance, fixed allowances, bonuses or commissions, overtime arrangements, work location, remote ratio, probationary period, notice period and proposed start date. The right to work of foreign candidates should be verified separately before onboarding.

5. Employment Contracts, Contract Types and Probationary Period

Open-ended contracts apply to long-term ongoing positions. Fixed-term contracts must have a temporary replacement, temporary project or other statutory temporary reason, with a cumulative term generally not exceeding 2 years; uncertain-term contracts apply to needs with a clear end event but uncertain date, generally up to 4 years. Ongoing positions cannot circumvent open-ended protection through repeated renewals.

The contract should at least include the parties, start date, work location, duties, contract term, wages and payment cycle, holiday and Christmas allowances, working hours, probationary period, leave, notice period, applicable collective agreement, bonuses or commissions, confidentiality, intellectual property, data processing and equipment arrangements.

Management or senior management position240 daysRequires genuine management duties and authority

During probation, either party may generally terminate without ordinary dismissal grounds or compensation, but anti-discrimination, retaliation and special protection restrictions still apply. If the employer terminates after probation has lasted more than 60 days, generally 7 days' notice is required; after more than 120 days, generally 30 days' notice is required; failure to comply will generate corresponding wage liability.

Independent contractor status depends on actual control, organizational integration, economic dependence, substitution rights and business risk. Where an individual works long-term with fixed schedules and daily direction, a misclassification review should be conducted.

6. Wages, Minimum Wage and Gross-to-Net Calculation

The wage determination sequence is: confirm the mainland, Azores or Madeira work region; check the applicable collective agreement, industry and job grade wages; compare with the regional statutory minimum wage and apply the higher; verify full-time or part-time ratio based on actual normal working hours; finally calculate basic salary, two annual allowances, overtime, bonuses, meal allowances and expense reimbursements separately.

The 2026 Portuguese mainland minimum wage is EUR 920/month, and the minimum annualized fixed wage reference is EUR 920 × 14 = EUR 12,880. Fourteen payments does not mean EUR 1,073.33 must be transferred every month; companies may pay the holiday allowance and Christmas allowance according to statutory rules and written arrangements, but cannot cancel the annual entitlement.

Example Calculation Result

20 hours/week part-time920 × 50%EUR 460 regular monthly wage, with the two allowances handled pro-rata
Monthly salary EUR 2,0002,000 × 14EUR 28,000 annual fixed gross wage

Reimbursements and genuine expense compensation cannot fill the minimum wage; overtime, night work, public holiday work and uncertain bonuses should be calculated outside the base salary. If the two allowances are paid monthly in twelfths, they should be itemized separately in the contract and payslip, and the combined transfer amount cannot be written entirely as basic salary.

The payslip should show gross wage, employee 11% social security, personal income tax withholding, two allowances, bonuses, absences and net pay. The employee's actual net pay depends on income, family status, resident status and region, and companies cannot promise net pay using a uniform tax rate.

7. Working Time, Overtime and Records

Standard full-time working time is generally 8 hours/day, 40 hours/week. Employees are generally entitled to 11 consecutive hours of daily rest and at least one day of weekly rest; applicable collective agreements, shifts and special work regimes may provide more favorable treatment.

Item General Rule Employer Action

Overtime on a rest day or public holidayGenerally 50% additionalAlso verify compensatory rest and industry rules
Night work or shiftsSubject to time, health and collective agreement constraintsDetermine specific shifts and allowances before quotation

Using a monthly gross wage of EUR 2,000 and a 40-hour work week as an example, for quotation demonstration, the hourly wage can be estimated as EUR 2,000 × 12 ÷ (52 × 40) ≈ EUR 11.54. The premium portion for the first overtime hour on a working day is approximately EUR 2.89, with total payment of approximately EUR 14.43; the final payslip must use Portuguese statutory formulas and applicable collective agreement parameters.

Employers should keep records of schedules, actual start and end times, rest, overtime approval and compensation. Whether remote work, training, travel, on-call and cross-time-zone meetings count as working time should be determined based on whether the employee is at the employer's disposal.

8. Statutory Holidays, Annual Leave and Other Statutory Leave

Employees in a full year are entitled to at least 22 working days of annual leave. In the year of onboarding, generally 2 working days are accrued per full contract month, up to 20 days, generally used after 6 months of work; this does not mean employees who have worked less than one year have no annual leave.

Carnival Tuesday and municipal holidays need separate verification; the common Lisbon municipal holiday is June 13. Holidays falling on weekends generally do not automatically generate compensatory rest; holiday work should be calculated for premium or compensatory rest based on industry, schedule and collective agreement.

Ordinary sick leave is generally paid by social security from the 4th day: generally 55% of reference wage for 30 days, 60% for 31–90 days, 70% for 91–365 days, and 75% after 365 days. Employers still need to verify temporary incapacity certificates, record absences, adjust wage items and check whether collective agreements or insurance require top-up.

Initial parental leave is basically 120 or 150 days, and may be extended by 30 days when sharing conditions are met. The 120-day option is generally paid at 100% of reference wage, the 150-day non-shared option is generally 80%, and the 180-day shared option is generally 83%, subject to social security approval.

10. Local Employees and Foreign Employees

Local employees, EU or EEA employees and other foreign employees in principle enjoy the same minimum protection under Portuguese labor law, but the right to work, tax residency and social security coverage may differ. Companies should verify the actual work location, permit status, tax number (NIF), social security number (NISS) and insurance coverage before onboarding.

Cross-border employees holding a valid A1 certificate or other social security coverage certificate may have changed Portuguese contribution obligations. An Employer of Record (EOR) cannot automatically grant a work permit, nor can it eliminate cross-border tax, permanent establishment or joint employment risks.

The separation of a foreign employee may affect residence or work permit status, and the contracting employer should coordinate labor law termination and immigration compliance, but cannot replace lawful termination procedures with permit risk.

11. Remote Work, Data Privacy and Employee Records

Remote work agreements should specify the primary work location, on-site frequency, equipment and costs, working hours, accident reporting, occupational health and safety, information security and return-to-office mechanisms. Before an employee works long-term from another country, tax residency, permanent establishment, social security, A1 certificate, data transfer and right to work should be assessed.

Employee data processing should follow lawful basis, transparency, necessity, retention period and security requirements. Employee consent should not be the sole basis for work monitoring; health, disciplinary, payroll and identity data should have restricted access.

Employers should keep contracts, payslips, attendance, overtime, leave, sick leave certificates, performance, discipline, insurance and separation materials, and manage them according to their respective statutory periods. System access should be promptly closed after separation and equipment and data handover completed.

12. Termination of Employment, Compensation and Offboarding Settlement

Outside the probationary period, employers cannot freely terminate without reason. Based on the facts, they should choose a statutory path such as disciplinary dismissal, position elimination, unsuitability or collective redundancy, and complete evidence, employee response, consultation, notice and compensation procedures. The end of a client project itself does not automatically constitute lawful grounds for termination.

Employer notice periods for economic termination are generally: 15 days for less than 1 year of service, 30 days for 1 to less than 5 years, 60 days for 5 to less than 10 years, and 75 days for 10 years or more. Compensation must be calculated segment by segment based on the contract start date and service period; relevant service periods formed after May 1, 2023 are often calculated at 14 days' basic salary plus seniority pay per year, while historical service periods may apply different standards.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

What is the 2026 Portuguese minimum wage?
The Portuguese mainland minimum monthly wage is EUR 920, effective January 1, 2026. The Azores and Madeira apply their own regional minimum wages, and companies should verify based on the employee's actual work location.

Should the Portuguese minimum wage be calculated on 12 or 14 months?
Portuguese employees generally receive one month's holiday allowance and one month's Christmas allowance in addition to 12 months' basic salary. Therefore, the annual fixed wage reference for the mainland minimum wage is EUR 920 × 14, i.e., EUR 12,880.

What are the general social security rates for Portuguese employers and employees?
For ordinary employees, the employer generally bears 23.75% and the employee bears 11%. The employee share is withheld from wages by the employer. Employee category, reductions and cross-border coverage may change the actual rate.

What is the maximum probationary period for an ordinary employee?
Generally 90 days for an ordinary open-ended employee; up to 180 days for complex technical, high-responsibility or special trust positions, and up to 240 days for management or senior management positions. It must be supported by actual job duties.

Is an employee entitled to annual leave before completing one year?
Yes. In the year of onboarding, generally 2 working days are accrued per full contract month, up to 20 days, generally used after 6 months of work. Accrued but unused entitlement should be settled upon separation.

Are 13th and 14th month pay mandatory in Portugal?
The commonly referred to 13th and 14th month pay correspond to the Christmas allowance and holiday allowance. Companies may pay them at statutory times or in monthly twelfths in accordance with the law and written agreement, but cannot cancel the annual entitlement.

Is economic dismissal compensation uniformly 14 days' wages per year?
No. Relevant service periods formed after May 1, 2023 are often calculated at 14 days per year, but historical service periods may apply different standards, and must be calculated segment by segment based on the contract start date and service period.

Can employees be hired without a Portuguese entity?
Companies may assess a compliant Employer of Record (EOR) arrangement, but must confirm the contract employer, collective agreement, social security, work accident insurance, right to work, actual management and termination responsibility case by case.
