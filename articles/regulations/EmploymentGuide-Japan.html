Labor Law, Minimum Wage, Social Insurance, Leave and Termination Compliance

2026 POLICY UPDATE

Association Health Insurance Rate Adjustment
From the March 2026 payroll period (usually paid in April), the Tokyo health insurance total rate decreases to 9.85%, and the national uniform long-term care insurance total rate increases to 1.62%. Employers should update payroll parameters by region and employee age.

Childcare Support Contribution Officially Collected
For general insured persons, from the April 2026 payroll period (May payment), a total rate of 0.23% applies. Employers should separately list labor and management shares and distinguish this from the employer-only 0.36% childcare contribution.

Employment Insurance Rate Reduced
From April 1, 2026, the general industry employment insurance total rate decreases to 1.35%, with employees bearing 0.5% and employers bearing 0.85%. Employers should switch rates in the April payroll cycle and confirm exceptions by industry.

Japan has a mature but highly procedural labor and social security system. When Chinese enterprises recruit employees in Japan, they cannot focus only on wage levels; they must also simultaneously handle work location, contract type, fixed overtime, working hours records, social insurance, annual leave, and dismissal reasons and procedures.

This guide is intended for Chinese enterprises planning to directly employ, use Employer of Record (EOR) services or conduct payroll calculation (Payroll) in Japan. The content is based on the 2026 v2.1 version of the material and has been reviewed against official parameters verifiable as of August 25, 2026. Specific implementation should still be confirmed in conjunction with the employee's location, age, position, insurance institution and company system.

When fixed-term contracts with the same employer exceed 5 years in aggregate, the employee may lawfully apply for conversion to an indefinite-term contract. For employees who have been renewed 3 or more times or worked continuously for more than 1 year, non-renewal on expiry usually requires at least 30 days' advance notice and explanation of reasons.

The probation period is usually 3-6 months, but probationary employees remain protected against dismissal. Employers should state the period and evaluation criteria in the contract, and wages must not be below the applicable minimum wage; termination still requires objectively reasonable grounds, evidence and applicable notice procedures.

6. Wages, Minimum Wage and Pre-tax Take-home Pay Calculation (Gross-to-Net)

Item Current Rules Verification Method
Tokyo Regional Minimum WageJPY 1,226/hour, effective from October 3, 2025Tokyo Labour Bureau
Other PrefecturesRegional standards differ, usually adjusted each autumnMHLW national list and local Labour Bureaux
Specific Industry Minimum WageSome industries may be higher than regional standardsApply the higher of the two by work location and industry
Monthly Salary ConversionExclude overtime, statutory holiday, late-night wages, bonuses and other non-includable items from comparable wages, then divide by average monthly scheduled hoursKeep conversion working paper

Example: A Tokyo operations specialist with a monthly salary of JPY 500,000, if roughly converted at 160 hours per month, is equivalent to JPY 3,125/hour, above the Tokyo minimum wage. If the monthly salary includes fixed overtime, normal wages and fixed overtime wages must still be separated, and normal wages themselves must reach the minimum wage.

Wages should in principle be paid in currency directly and in full to the employee, at least once a month and on a fixed payment date. Payslips should break down basic wage, allowances, fixed overtime, excess overtime, late-night/holiday wages, social insurance and taxes. Without statutory basis, labor-management agreement or valid authorization, equipment losses, training fees or handover "penalties" must not be arbitrarily deducted from final wages.

Full-time employees with 6 months of continuous employment and 80% attendance usually acquire 10 days of paid annual leave at once, then gradually increase to 20 days by years of service. Employers must ensure employees with 10 or more days of annual leave actually use at least 5 days per year. Part-time employees are granted pro-rata based on weekly scheduled working days and annual working days, and cannot simply be multiplied by full-time days by hourly ratio.

Example: An employee who joins on January 15, 2026 and meets attendance conditions usually acquires 10 days on July 15, 2026, rather than accruing 1.67 days per month; the company may provide more favorable treatment through contract or a unified grant system. Japan does not have a generally applicable mandatory cash buyout rule for unused annual leave on departure; priority should be given to arranging leave before departure and verifying company rules.

11. Remote Work, Data Privacy and Record Keeping

Remote work in Japan still requires objective working hours records, overtime approval and occupational health and safety management. My Number, health check, bank account, payroll and dependent information should be authorized by sensitivity level, with access and change records retained.

When employees work remotely long-term in other prefectures, minimum wage should usually be verified by actual main work location. Cross-border remote work also requires separate assessment of the employee's location labor law, tax, social insurance, immigration and permanent establishment risks.

In principle, 30 days' advance notice of dismissal should be given; for each day short, usually 1 day's average wage notice allowance must be paid. Average wage should be calculated by statutory method and cannot uniformly be monthly salary divided by 30. When the employee requests, departure wages and money or items within the scope of rights should in principle be handled within 7 days; disputed and undisputed amounts should be separated.

Settlement example: Tokyo 35-year-old employee with monthly salary JPY 500,000, employer intends ordinary dismissal on the same day; statutory average wage already calculated by payroll system as JPY 16,000/day, 15 days worked in the month, plus approved reimbursement of JPY 20,000, company has no additional retirement allowance or annual leave buyout system.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Does Japan have a national uniform minimum wage?
There is no single national amount. Minimum wage is determined by prefecture, and some industries have higher specific minimum wages; employers should usually apply the higher of the two standards.

What is the minimum wage implemented in Tokyo in 2026?
As of August 25, 2026, Tokyo's current regional minimum wage is JPY 1,226/hour, effective from October 3, 2025; it should be updated again after the next formal adjustment takes effect.

What are the standard daily and weekly working hours for Japanese employees?
In principle, 8 hours/day and 40 hours/week. Statutory overtime usually requires a compliant basis such as a 36 Agreement and payment of the corresponding premium.

Are Japanese national holidays all paid leave for private enterprise employees?
No. National holidays do not automatically equal additional paid leave for private enterprise employees; judgment should be combined with contract, work rules, scheduling and statutory holiday arrangements.

Can employees be dismissed at any time during probation?
No. Probation termination still requires objectively reasonable grounds, social appropriateness, evidence and applicable procedures.

Does paying 30 days' wages make a dismissal lawful?
No. 30-day notice or notice allowance mainly addresses the minimum notice procedure and cannot replace a lawful, reasonable dismissal reason.

Must all unused annual leave be cashed out on departure?
Japan does not have a generally applicable mandatory cash buyout rule. Priority should be given to arranging leave before departure and verifying the contract, work rules or company commitment.

Does Japanese employer cost in 2026 only need to add Employees' Pension 9.15%?
No. Health insurance, applicable long-term care insurance, childcare support contribution, employment insurance, work injury insurance, childcare contribution, and overtime and company benefits should also be considered.

Can a Chinese company without a Japanese entity use Employer of Record services?
It can be assessed, but the lawful employment entity, social insurance, actual management, dispatch restrictions and foreign employee issues must be confirmed case by case; it cannot be presumed that all arrangements are compliant simply by the name "EOR."
