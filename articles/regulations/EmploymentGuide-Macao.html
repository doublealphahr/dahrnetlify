Minimum Wage, Social Security, Leave and Termination Compliance

2026 POLICY UPDATE

Minimum Wage Fully Increased
The monthly minimum wage rises to MOP 7,280 from January 1, 2026, with other wage systems adjusted accordingly.

Holiday Calendar Classified Implementation
The statutory mandatory holidays for private employees remain 10 days; the government's full-year public holidays cannot all be directly applied.

Professional Tax Threshold Included in Payroll
Employees with monthly income exceeding MOP 16,000 need to enter withholding assessment and complete payment quarterly.

As more Chinese enterprises recruit for office, retail, hotel, customer service and professional positions in Macao, local employment management is no longer just signing employment contracts and paying wages. Enterprises also need to simultaneously handle employee status, minimum wage, working hours and scheduling, mandatory holidays, annual leave, Social Security Fund (FSS) contributions, professional tax, work accident insurance and termination compensation.

Macao's employment rules treat resident and non-resident employees, indefinite-term and fixed-term contracts, long-term employees and casual workers differently. The government's full-year public holidays do not equal all statutory mandatory holidays enjoyed by private employees. If an enterprise only estimates cost at "monthly salary plus MOP 60 social security," it is easy to omit insurance, holiday work, non-resident employment fee, housing, return travel and potential dismissal compensation.

This guide is intended for Chinese enterprises and HR, legal, finance, payroll and overseas business teams planning to recruit employees in Macao, systematically organizing the employment contracts, probation period, minimum wage, working hours and leave, resident and non-resident employee costs, professional tax, departure settlement and Employer of Record (EOR) rules that need to be mastered in 2026, helping enterprises complete the first round of compliance judgment before recruitment, quotation, payroll and employee exit.

2. Three Rules to Watch Most in 2026

2.1 Minimum Wage Fully Increased from January 1, 2026

Macao minimum wage standards are adjusted from January 1, 2026 as follows:

Wage System 2026 Minimum Wage
MonthlyMOP 7,280/month
WeeklyMOP 1,680/week
DailyMOP 280/day
HourlyMOP 35/hour
Piece-rate or commissionAverage MOP 35/hour

The new standard applies simultaneously to eligible local and foreign employees. Minimum wage mainly compares the employee's basic remuneration; overtime pay, night shift or shift additional remuneration, 13th month salary and similar periodic benefits cannot be used to make up the difference.

Domestic work employees and disabled employees are not within the general scope of the minimum wage law. Non-resident employees' free accommodation or minimum MOP 500 housing allowance also cannot be counted toward minimum wage.

Enterprises should update all wage system parameters from the January 2026 payroll. Wage periods crossing December 2025 and January 2026 should be compared separately before and after the effective date.

2.2 2026 Must Distinguish Private Mandatory Holidays from Government Public Holidays

The Macao government has announced the 2026 public holidays, public administration staff exemption from work and compensatory holiday dates, but private employees under the Labor Relations Law still have only 10 statutory mandatory holidays.

Government public holidays such as Good Friday, Buddha's Birthday, Tuen Ng Festival and Christmas do not automatically become statutory paid holidays for all private employees unless the employment contract, company policy or other applicable arrangements provide more favorable treatment.

Enterprises should separately establish in payroll and scheduling systems:

  1. Private employee mandatory holidays;
  2. Company additional holidays;
  3. Government public holidays;
  4. Public administration staff compensatory holidays.

When mandatory holidays overlap with weekly rest, weekly rest must still be arranged separately.

2.3 Professional Tax Threshold and Quarterly Payment Rhythm Need to Be Included in Payroll Process

The Macao government professional tax service page confirms in 2026:

  1. When employee monthly income exceeds MOP 16,000, the employer should enter professional tax withholding assessment;
  2. When part-time employees' daily wages and other taxable income exceed MOP 640, withholding assessment is also required;
  3. Professional tax already withheld in the previous quarter should usually be paid before January 15, April 15, July 15 and October 15.

Professional tax and FSS contributions are two independent obligations. Completing FSS filing does not mean professional tax has been handled.

Enterprises should establish employee tax files at onboarding, identify the taxable nature of basic wages, bonuses, commissions and benefits in kind monthly, and complete payment and reconciliation quarterly.

At onboarding, complete:

  1. Identity, address, bank account and work rights verification;
  2. Written employment contract in a language the employee understands;
  3. Job description, wage structure and scheduling rules;
  4. Probation, normal working hours, weekly rest, holiday and annual leave agreement;

  6. Non-resident employee permit and employment fee verification;
  7. Work accident insurance;
  8. Privacy, confidentiality, intellectual property and equipment documents;
  9. Employee handbook, payslips and overtime approval process.

Fixed-term contracts must state the specific facts supporting the temporary need. If the position is long-term and mechanically renewed each year, it may be deemed an indefinite-term contract.

The employment contract should at least state the parties, work location, duties, wages, allowances, bonuses, commissions, normal working hours, shifts, weekly rest, overtime, holidays, annual leave, probation, notice period, data protection and termination rules.

6. Probation Period Management

The probation period for general indefinite-term contracts is presumed to be 90 days, and for fixed-term contracts 30 days; the parties may cancel or adjust by written agreement.

Employee Category Probation Period Cap
Ordinary employeeUsually maximum 90 days
Highly technical, special qualification, leadership or management positionsUsually maximum 180 days
Fixed-term contract employeeUsually maximum 30 days

During probation, employment relationship may usually be terminated with simplified procedures, but note:

  1. Contract may provide more favorable notice;
  2. When actual probation exceeds 90 days, usually at least 7 days' notice;
  3. Must not terminate based on discrimination, retaliation, pregnancy or work injury status;
  4. Must settle wages, overtime and proportional annual leave already accrued;
  5. Payables should in principle be paid within 9 working days after termination.

If work continues after probation expires, service years are calculated continuously. Employers must not continue to use "failed probation" to handle employees who have already been confirmed.

7. Wages, Minimum Wage and Wage Payment

When comparing minimum wage, distinguish basic remuneration from other items:

Wage Item Whether Counted Toward Minimum Wage

Fixed food or position allowanceNeeds judgment based on fixed nature and legal definition
Overtime payNo
Night shift or shift additional remunerationNo
13th month salary or similar periodic benefitNo
Non-resident housing allowanceNo
Expense reimbursementNo

Minimum Wage and Overtime Example
Assume a monthly salaried employee's wage is MOP 7,280; the statutory normal hourly basic remuneration reference is:
MOP 7,280 ÷ 30 ÷ 8 = approximately MOP 30.33.

If the employee voluntarily works overtime, the reference hourly wage increased by 20% is:
MOP 30.33 × 1.2 = approximately MOP 36.40.

If it is a statutory overtime situation arranged by the employer involuntarily, the reference hourly wage increased by 50% is:
MOP 30.33 × 1.5 = approximately MOP 45.50.

MOP 35 minimum hourly wage cannot be directly used as the overtime base for all monthly salaried employees.

11. Remote Work, Data Privacy and Employee Records

Remote work does not change the employer's responsibility for working hours, wages, occupational safety and employee data. The contract or policy should at least state:

  1. Employee's usual location;
  2. Working hours and attendance;
  3. Equipment and expenses;
  4. Accident reporting;
  5. Data access and confidentiality;
  6. Intellectual property;
  7. Cross-border data processing;
  8. Work location change approval.

When employees work long-term in Mainland China, Hong Kong or other countries and regions, the actual work location's labor law, tax, social security and data requirements should be re-assessed. A Macao employment contract cannot cover or exclude mandatory provisions of the actual work location.

Employee files, payslips, attendance, consent records and termination documents should be kept at least according to statutory periods.

12.3 Just Cause and Disciplinary Procedure

"Just cause" requires serious facts that make the employment relationship impossible to continue; ordinary poor performance cannot be directly equated with serious breach.

The party claiming just cause should notify the other party in writing within 30 days of learning the relevant facts, specifying the facts.

Employers should handle in the following order:

  1. Investigate facts;
  2. Preserve evidence;
  3. Give the employee a reasonable opportunity to respond;
  4. Verify whether the employee is protected;
  5. Form specific written reasons within 30 days;
  6. Assess whether the sanction is appropriate;
  7. Decide on warning, other disciplinary action or termination;
  8. Issue termination and settlement documents.

12.5 Departure Settlement Example

Assume a Macao resident employee with monthly basic wage MOP 20,000, having passed probation, 4 years and 6 months of service, employer dismisses immediately without just cause, 4 days of remaining annual leave:

Item Calculation Amount
Final wagesMOP 20,000 ÷ 30 × 15 daysMOP 10,000.00
15 days' payment in lieu of noticeMOP 20,000 ÷ 30 × 15 daysMOP 10,000.00
Seniority compensation4.5 years × 13 days × MOP 20,000 ÷ 30MOP 39,000.00
Unused annual leave4 days × MOP 20,000 ÷ 30MOP 2,666.67
Total gross payableTotalMOP 61,666.67

Payable wages and compensation should in principle be paid within 9 working days after termination of the employment relationship.

If a fixed-term contract is terminated early with 6 months remaining, monthly salary MOP 20,000, the remaining term compensation reference is:
6 × 3 days × MOP 20,000 ÷ 30 = MOP 12,000.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

What is the minimum wage in Macao in 2026?
The monthly minimum wage is MOP 7,280, weekly MOP 1,680, daily MOP 280, hourly MOP 35. The average hourly basic remuneration of piece-rate or commission employees must also not be lower than MOP 35.

Are all Macao government public holidays statutory paid holidays for private employees?
No. Statutory mandatory holidays for private employees are 10 days. Whether other government public holidays are taken depends on the employment contract, company policy or other applicable arrangements.

What is the employer social security cost for Macao resident employees?
Long-term resident employees usually require the employer to contribute MOP 60 per month and the employee to contribute MOP 30 per month. Work accident insurance is a separate mandatory employer cost.

Do non-resident employees participate in FSS?
Non-resident employees usually do not participate in mandatory FSS, but the employer usually needs to bear the employment fee, housing or housing allowance, work accident insurance and return travel after termination of the employment relationship.

Can employees be dismissed at any time during probation?
Notice and compensation rules during probation are relatively simplified, but the probation period, contract agreement and employee protection status still need to be checked. When actual probation exceeds 90 days, usually at least 7 days' notice is required.

Does paying 15 days' wages necessarily allow dismissal of an employee?
No. 15 days is only one of the notice baselines when the contract has no more favorable provision and the employer dismisses an indefinite-term contract without just cause. The employer also needs to calculate seniority compensation and verify protection status.

Does Macao have a statutory 13th month salary applicable to all employees?
There is no unified statutory 13th month salary applicable to all employees. However, if the employment contract, company policy or stable practice has formed a 13th month salary right, the employer still needs to fulfill it.

Can Chinese enterprises employ Macao non-resident employees through EOR?
It can be assessed, but the employer, position, work location and permit must be confirmed case by case to be consistent. Using EOR cannot automatically obtain, transfer or renew a non-resident employee permit.
