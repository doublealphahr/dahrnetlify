Minimum Wage, CCT, Social Security, Leave, and Termination

2026 POLICY UPDATE

Minimum wage increase: Effective January 1, 2026, the monthly minimum wage will increase to NTD 29,500, and the hourly minimum wage to NTD 196.

Income tax parameter adjustments: For the 115th year, the general exemption will increase to NTD 101,000, the single standard deduction to NTD 136,000, and the special deduction for salary income to NTD 227,000.

Full implementation of 16 national holidays: Private enterprises must re-verify Lunar New Year, restored holidays, make-up holidays, and national holiday work wages.

Taiwan, China, is suitable for standardized local employment and Employer of Record (EOR) services, but companies cannot estimate costs solely based on "salary plus 6% labor pension." Full employer costs also involve labor insurance, employment insurance, occupational accident insurance, national health insurance, labor pension, overtime, unused special leave wages, and termination costs.

From January 1, 2026, the monthly minimum wage will increase to NTD 29,500 and the hourly minimum wage to NTD 196; the 2026 comprehensive income tax exemption, deductions, and tax brackets will be adjusted simultaneously. 2026 will also apply 16 national holidays, and private enterprises must correctly handle the relationship between national holidays, regular days off, rest days, and make-up holidays.

This guide is aimed at Chinese enterprises recruiting and managing employees in Taiwan, China, covering recruitment and onboarding, contract types, probation, wages, working hours, leave, labor and health insurance, labor pension, taxation, local and foreign employees, remote work, termination of employment, as well as entity, EOR, and payroll outsourcing models. When involving mainland China investment entities, foreign or mainland Chinese employees, sensitive industries, and work permits, separate verification should be conducted.

Enterprises especially cannot understand the probation period as a period of no-liability termination, nor can they circumvent labor protection for continuous positions through fixed-term contracts, dispatch, or contracting arrangements.

2. Three Rules to Watch Most in 2026

2.1 Minimum Wage Increased to Monthly NTD 29,500

Effective January 1, 2026, the monthly minimum wage will increase from NTD 28,590 to NTD 29,500, and the hourly minimum wage from NTD 190 to NTD 196.

Enterprises should simultaneously update:

  1. Offer letters and labor contracts;
  2. Hourly wages for part-time and working students;
  3. Normal wage and overtime pay base;
  4. Labor insurance insured salary brackets;
  5. Occupational accident insurance insured salary brackets;
  6. Labor pension monthly contribution wages;
  7. Wage structure for low-wage employees.

The minimum wage is the minimum remuneration during normal working hours and does not include overtime pay, rest day work wages, and national holiday work wages.

2.2 115th Year Income Tax Parameters Increased

The relevant parameters for the 115th year comprehensive income tax have been adjusted:

Item 115th Year Amount
General exemptionNTD 101,000
Exemption for persons over 70, spouse, or dependent lineal ascendantsNTD 151,500
Single standard deductionNTD 136,000
Standard deduction for married personsNTD 272,000
Special deduction for salary incomeNTD 227,000
Special deduction for persons with disabilitiesNTD 227,000

Enterprises should update the 2026 salary income withholding data. The final tax burden of employees still depends on tax resident status, number of dependents, income type, and personal deduction items, and a fixed income tax rate cannot be uniformly applied.

2.3 16 National Holidays Enter Full-Year Implementation

2026 applies 16 national memorial days and festivals that should be observed, including 5 days for Lunar New Year, as well as the restored Confucius' Birthday, Taiwan Restoration and Kinmen Guningtou Victory Memorial Day, and Constitution Day.

Private enterprises should first determine the regular days off and rest days in each 7-day cycle for employees, and then handle:

  1. National holidays;
  2. National holidays coinciding with regular days off or rest days;
  3. Make-up holidays;
  4. Holiday shifts with employee consent;
  5. National holiday work wages.

The government administrative agency office calendar cannot be directly equated with the private enterprise employee calendar.

5.2 Probation

Taiwan, China labor law does not have a "statutory probation period" that can independently exclude the Labor Standards Act. Enterprises can agree on a probation or assessment period in the labor contract, commonly 1 to 3 months in practice, but must still comply with wage, working hours, insurance, and termination rules.

Scenario Handling Method Main Risk
Onboarding probation agreementContract specifies term, job requirements, performance standards, and assessment mechanismWages must not be lower than minimum wage, insurance must be handled upon onboarding
Employer termination during probationMust still have reasonable and lawful grounds, and handle advance notice and severance payMust not only write "probation failed" to circumvent statutory termination
Employee resignation during probationHandled according to contract, work rules, and Labor Standards ActWages and overtime must still be settled
Continued work after probation expiresContinue performance according to original contractSimultaneously confirm salary, job grade, and benefits

Probation is not a cost-free termination period. Before termination, the employee's protection status, termination grounds, evidence, advance notice period, severance pay, and unused special leave should be verified.

Item 2026 Rules

The wage structure should separately list base salary, duty allowance, fixed allowance, bonus, commission, overtime pay, meal or transportation subsidies.

Genuine expense reimbursements, non-fixed benefits, and overtime pay should not be used to make up the minimum wage.

Assume a Taipei part-time operations assistant works 80 hours in January 2026, the normal working hours wage floor is:

Overtime, rest days, and national holiday work should be calculated separately, and NTD 15,680 cannot be used as a lump-sum wage.

8.1 Special Leave

Statutory special leave generally only generates 3 days when work reaches 6 months. When an employee leaves before 6 months, there is generally no statutory special leave cash-out, but wages, overtime, bonuses, and commissions must still be settled.

When the company adopts a calendar year system, leave can be granted in advance proportionally, but when the employee leaves, the result calculated back according to the actual arrival date must not be lower than the statutory standard.

The Labor Standards Act requires employees to be off on national holidays with pay. When shifting with the employee's consent, the employer must specify which working day it is shifted to.

When an employee works on a national holiday with consent, wages should generally be doubled according to law. Part-time and working students also enjoy corresponding rights.

The government administrative agency 2026 office calendar shows a total of 120 days off throughout the year and multiple consecutive holidays, but this calendar cannot be directly applied to private enterprises. Private enterprises should form their own work calendar based on employees' regular days off, rest days, and national holidays.

  1. Labor Standards Act responsibilities;

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the 2026 minimum wage in Taiwan, China?
Effective January 1, 2026, the monthly minimum wage is NTD 29,500, and the hourly minimum wage is NTD 196. Part-time and working students must also not be lower than the applicable hourly wage standard.

Is the employer cost just wage plus 6% labor pension?
No. Employers should also bear labor insurance, employment insurance, occupational accident insurance, national health insurance, and possible supplementary premiums, and consider overtime, unused special leave, and termination costs.

Is there a statutory probation period in Taiwan, China?
Labor law does not have an independent statutory probation period that can exclude the Labor Standards Act. Enterprises can agree on a probation or assessment period, but during probation, wages must still be paid according to law, insurance must be handled, and labor relations must be terminated according to statutory grounds and procedures.

How long does an employee work before enjoying special leave?
Employees who have worked for 6 months but less than 1 year enjoy 3 days of special leave; those who have worked for 1 year but less than 2 years enjoy 7 days, increasing according to seniority thereafter, up to a maximum of 30 days.

How many national holidays do private enterprises have in 2026?
There are currently 16 national memorial days and festivals that should be observed. Private enterprises should distinguish between national holidays, regular days off, rest days, and make-up holidays, and handle holiday shifts and work wages according to law.

How do employers calculate health insurance costs?
The general health insurance rate is 5.17%, but the employer cost for employed persons also involves the 60% sharing ratio and the average number of dependents, and cannot simply be calculated as insured amount multiplied by 5.17% and then by 60%. The second-generation health insurance supplementary premium should also be judged separately.

Can an employee be directly dismissed by paying pay in lieu of notice?
No. Employer termination of an indefinite-term labor contract must have statutory grounds. Pay in lieu of notice only handles the advance notice period and will not remedy unlawful termination grounds or procedures.

Can employees be hired through EOR in Taiwan, China?
It can be evaluated, but the actual legal employer, client management boundary, labor and health insurance and labor pension responsibilities, as well as employee identity, work permit, and mainland China investment background must be confirmed.
