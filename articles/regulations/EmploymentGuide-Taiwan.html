Minimum Wage, CCT, Social Security, Leave, and Termination

2026 POLICY UPDATE

RIFL new employment incentive launched: Eligible private employers may use code 710 to file RIFL for qualifying new hires between May 1, 2026 and April 30, 2027; the benefit may last up to 48 months, but eligibility must be verified per person.

FAL implementation postponed to November 1: The Labor Assistance Fund (FAL) takes effect from November 1, 2026, generally 1% for large companies and 2.5% for MiPyMEs, with corresponding social security contributions for applicable non-RIFL employment relationships reduced equally.

2026 SMVM staged increase completed: The national minimum living wage rises to ARS 376,600/month and ARS 1,883/hour from August 1, 2026; employers must still check whether the CCT provides a higher wage.

When hiring employees in Argentina, companies cannot only compare with the national minimum living wage (SMVM). Most positions are also bound by collective labor agreements (CCT), and position category, basic wage, attendance bonus, seniority allowance, union items, working hours, and some termination parameters may be determined by the CCT.

Argentina's minimum wage, collective agreement wages, and social security parameters update frequently. Before quoting and signing contracts, Chinese companies should simultaneously lock the province and city of work, actual duties, CCT, company size and MiPyME status, workers' compensation (ART) rate, social medical insurance (obra social), and ARCA filing parameters.

2. Three Rules to Watch Most in 2026

SMVM staged increase completed

First, the national minimum living wage (SMVM) is increased in stages in 2026. June 2026 is ARS 367,800, July is ARS 372,400, and from August it is ARS 376,600. SMVM is only a cross-industry reference floor; if the CCT wage table stipulates a higher position wage or additional items, the company must apply the CCT standard.

RIFL new employment incentive

Second, the Labor Regularization Incentive (RIFL) applies to eligible private employers and new hires. The qualifying onboarding window is May 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027, using mode code 710 for filing, and the benefit may apply for up to 48 months. Employers must verify prior employment status and statutory eligibility per person, and cannot apply the benefit by default to all new employees.

FAL postponed to November 1

Third, the Labor Assistance Fund (FAL) is postponed to take effect on November 1, 2026. Large companies generally contribute 1% and MiPyMEs generally 2.5% to FAL, with corresponding social security contributions for non-RIFL employment relationships reduced equally. FAL changes contribution items and termination fund management, but insufficient account balance does not reduce the employer's full statutory payment responsibility to employees.

3. Argentina Labor Law and Regulatory Framework

Argentina employment is mainly governed by the Labor Contract Law (LCT 20.744), Working Hours Law (Ley 11.544), Labor Modernization Law (Ley 27.802), social security, medical insurance, workers' compensation, and applicable CCT. Main institutions include the labor authority, Customs and Tax Administration (ARCA), National Social Security Administration (ANSES), and Superintendency of Labor Risks (SRT).

Federal labor law applies nationwide, but local taxes, registration practice, ART risk, and labor market vary by province and city. CCT is usually determined by the employer's main business activity and the employee's actual duties, and cannot be selected only by English job title or client industry. Office administrative or sales positions may also fall under the commercial industry CCT 130/75, but this should still be judged case by case based on the legal employer's activity.

An employment relationship is centered on actual subordinate management, personal service, and remuneration. Calling a person a "consultant" or signing a service agreement cannot eliminate the reclassification risk brought by fixed scheduling, exclusive service, direct performance management, and continuous monthly payment.

4. Recruitment, Offer, and Onboarding

Recruitment Advertisement Notes

Recruitment advertisements should state work location, contract type, working hours, gross or net wage basis, bonus and commission structure, and contract employer. Companies must not implement unreasonable screening based on political opinion, religion, union activity, gender, sexual orientation, nationality, disability, etc.

Probation for indefinite-term contracts is generally 6 months. Applicable CCT may extend probation to up to 8 months for companies with 6 to 100 employees, and up to 1 year for companies with up to 5 employees, but companies cannot automatically adopt the longer period based only on headcount; they must confirm that the CCT has made a valid provision.

Probationary employees still enjoy wages, social security, work injury and non-work injury illness protection, union rights, proportional SAC, and annual leave. Employees must be registered from day one; employers that fail to register generally cannot claim probation protection, and the same employee cannot be placed on probation repeatedly.

The wage floor judgment order is: determine the CCT corresponding to the employer activity and employee duties, check the latest CCT wage table, position category, and additional items, then compare with the SMVM of the month and apply the higher standard. CCT salary adjustments may take effect in stages or retroactively, so quotations should also check the next effective date.

Assume a Buenos Aires administrative employee has a monthly gross wage of ARS 1,000,000. This wage is above the August 2026 SMVM, but it still needs to be confirmed whether the commercial industry CCT or other agreement stipulates a higher basic wage, attendance bonus, seniority allowance, or fixed supplement. SAC, annual leave pay, overtime, and termination compensation cannot be used to fill the basic wage gap.

Monthly salaried employees are generally paid monthly. The payslip should state employer, employee, payment period, position and CCT category, pre-tax items, employee deductions, net wage, and necessary employer contribution information. Commission, bonus, and overtime should be judged as to whether they are wage items and correctly enter social security, SAC, annual leave, and dismissal bases.

Employees who have not reached full annual qualification generally acquire 1 day of annual leave per 20 working days. Annual leave is generally scheduled between October 1 and April 30 of the following year, with at least 30 days written notice. 2026 rules allow the parties to agree on splitting, but each segment generally must not be less than 7 days. Monthly salaried employees' holiday pay is generally calculated as monthly salary divided by 25; proportional unused leave should be settled at departure.

Employer paid period for non-work injury illness is generally 3 to 12 months depending on seniority and family burden. 10. Local Employees and Expatriate or Foreign Employees

Local employees should complete CUIL, ARCA, obra social, ART, and applicable CCT registration. Foreign employees also need to confirm residence and work authorization before onboarding; an employment contract or EOR arrangement does not automatically generate work eligibility.

Cross-border posting should simultaneously assess tax residence, social security, wage payment, permanent establishment, benefit coordination, and immigration status. Long-term cross-province work by employees may also change local taxes, ART risk, or CCT practice, so remote location and actual service location must be kept updated.

After probation ends, without-cause dismissal seniority compensation is generally 1 month of best monthly normal and regular wage per service year or fraction over 3 months. Calculation applies CCT compensation ceiling, but result generally must not be less than 67% of the employee's normal monthly wage basis, and total compensation must not be less than 1 month wage.

Employer notice period is generally: 1 month for seniority up to 5 years, 2 months for over 5 years. Current rules generally do not require notice during probation. When employee is pregnant, union representative, discriminated against, or other protected status, special procedure verification should be conducted first.

Assume employee monthly wage ARS 1,000,000, service 2 years 7 months, without-cause dismissal on July 15 without notice: seniority compensation example ARS 3,000,000, payment in lieu of notice ARS 1,000,000, month-end integration approximately ARS 533,333, plus worked wages, proportional SAC and unused annual leave, example total approximately ARS 5,374,806. Formal amount still needs recalculation based on CCT, wage composition, termination date, and protected status.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the 2026 Argentina minimum wage?
From August 1, 2026, the national minimum living wage (SMVM) is ARS 376,600/month or ARS 1,883/hour. However, most positions still need to check the applicable CCT wage table, and apply the higher amount of SMVM and CCT standard.

What is the employer social security rate in Argentina?
Social security contributions are generally 18% or 20.4%, plus generally 6% obra social, variable-rate ART, mandatory life insurance, and applicable CCT or industry funds. Therefore a single percentage cannot represent the complete employer cost.

What deductions do Argentine employees generally bear?
Employee basic withholding generally includes retirement 11%, INSSJP 3%, and obra social 3%, plus personal income tax, union dues, and CCT-stipulated items.

Is 13th month pay mandatory in Argentina?
Yes. Supplementary annual salary (SAC) is generally paid twice in June and December, each calculated based on 50% of the highest monthly normal wage in the corresponding half-year.

What is the maximum probation period in Argentina?
Generally 6 months for indefinite-term contracts. Applicable CCT may extend to up to 8 months for companies with 6 to 100 employees, and up to 1 year for companies with up to 5 employees, but a valid CCT extension clause must be confirmed.

How many statutory annual leave days does Argentina have?
14, 21, 28, or 35 calendar days by seniority. When full annual qualification is not met, generally 1 day of annual leave per 20 working days.

How is without-cause dismissal compensation calculated in Argentina?
Generally 1 month of best monthly normal and regular wage per service year or fraction over 3 months, plus possible payment in lieu of notice, month-end integration, proportional SAC, unused annual leave, and other earned entitlements.

Can a company hire employees without an Argentine entity?
Companies may evaluate a compliant Employer of Record (EOR), but should confirm CCT, ARCA registration, obra social, ART, work eligibility, client management boundary, and termination responsibility case by case.
