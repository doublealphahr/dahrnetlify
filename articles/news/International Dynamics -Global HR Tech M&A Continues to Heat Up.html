June Human Resources Service Industry Milestones | International Dynamics: Global HR Tech M&A Continues to Heat Up

In June 2026, the human resources service industry reached a critical juncture marked by the implementation of the "15th Five-Year Plan" for employment, accelerated deep integration of AI and HR, and continued active global HR tech M&A.

From a global perspective, giants like Workday and Riviera Partners are accelerating M&A in the AI recruitment track, while BIPO secured a $50 million strategic investment. Below is a timeline of the month's industry milestones.

The 2026 Global Human Capital Trends Report Continues to Spark Industry Discussion

Deloitte emphasized in its 2026 Global Human Capital Trends Report that the acquisition of competitive advantage is shifting from "the allocation of talent within a static organizational structure" to "the dynamic orchestration of people, skills, and work." The report's survey shows that 70% of business leaders view "rapid and flexible adaptation" as their primary competitive strategy for the next three years. The report emphasizes that sustained competitive advantage stems from "human advantage" (such as judgment and creativity), supported by dynamic collaboration, digital trust, clear accountability, and a resilient culture. Another report simultaneously released by Deloitte shows that only about half of CEOs view HR as a true strategic partner, but organizations that place HR at their core see performance improvements of up to 34%. Additionally, Deloitte, in collaboration with Raconteur, surveyed 822 global business leaders to distill six key demands for building a truly agile workforce.

June 1: Workday Named a Leader in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Talent Acquisition

Workday announced it has been named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Talent Acquisition. Workday believes this recognition highlights its leadership in helping organizations modernize recruitment through connected data, AI, and agent-driven workflows.

June 9: South Korea's Codeit Acquires WhatTime to Build an AI Recruitment Platform

Codeit, a South Korean AI talent infrastructure company, completed the acquisition of recruitment operations solution provider WhatTime's "RoundHR," aiming to build an integrated AI recruitment platform. The specific acquisition price was not disclosed; this is Codeit's first strategic M&A ahead of its KOSDAQ listing. Codeit plans to integrate its AI interview solution "KADE" with WhatTime's recruitment management platform, consolidating the entire recruitment process—including talent sourcing, candidate management, AI interviews, assessment, and final screening—into a unified system. Codeit's revenue grew from 4.1 billion KRW in 2023 to 30.7 billion KRW in 2025, a more than sevenfold increase in two years.

June 17: BIPO Secures $50 Million Strategic Investment from Apis Partners

BIPO, a leading Asia-Pacific payroll and HR solutions provider, announced a $50 million strategic investment from global private equity firm Apis Partners. This investment is the ninth from Apis Growth Fund III, which recently completed its final close at $1.23 billion.

Founded in 2010 and headquartered in Singapore for the Asia-Pacific region, BIPO provides integrated solutions including HR management systems, global payroll outsourcing, Employer of Record (EOR) services, and workforce data analytics. BIPO has over 50 wholly-owned entities globally, serving more than 5,600 corporate clients and processing nearly $2 billion in payroll disbursements annually across over 170 countries and regions. BIPO stated it will continue to double down on AI, deepening the integration of Agentic workflows in project implementation and service delivery, to advance its strategic goal of becoming an "AI-deeply-embedded Asian payroll and HR platform."

June 18: Gartner Survey Shows Job Offer Acceptance Rate Dropped to 48%

A Gartner survey shows that only 48% of job seekers accepted their most recent job offer in Q4 2025, a significant decline from 54% in Q4 2024 and 85% in Q4 2023. Gartner's survey of 3,072 employees found that 30% of employees said they prefer to stay in their current roles due to economic uncertainty, even if they receive a better offer. High-skilled employees are 39% more likely to stay in their current roles than low-skilled employees. Jamie Kohn, Senior Director Analyst in Gartner's HR Practice, noted: "Overall hiring volume is down, but companies are still hiring for a few critical and harder-to-fill roles. Meanwhile, job seekers are now more reluctant to change jobs."

June 30: Korn Ferry Announces $1.1 Billion Acquisition of AMS

Korn Ferry, a leading global talent and organizational consulting firm, announced a definitive agreement with OMERS Private Equity to acquire UK-headquartered AMS for $1.1 billion. This is one of the largest talent industry deals in recent years.

Founded by Rosaleen Blair in 1996, AMS has deep expertise in Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO), early careers and campus recruitment, flexible workforce solutions, consulting, and skills development, with operations covering more than 120 countries. Following the merger, the two companies will have over 16,000 employees. Korn Ferry CEO Gary D. Burnison stated: "Despite all the technological innovations of yesterday, today, and tomorrow, the true driver of organizational success is people." AMS Founder and Chair Rosaleen Blair said: "At Korn Ferry, we have found like-minded partners who share the same beliefs and values."
