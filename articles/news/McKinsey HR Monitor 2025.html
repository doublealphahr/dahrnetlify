McKinsey: HR Monitor 2025

Five Core Challenges Facing HR in Europe and the US, and Strategies to Address Them

 

Published: 2025-07-23 10:54

The "McKinsey HR Monitor 2025" focuses on HR trends in Europe and the United States. Through data research on 1,925 enterprises and more than 4,000 employees, it reveals key HR management issues and optimization directions.

The report points out that HR currently faces five core challenges. First, workforce planning lacks strategic focus: 73% of enterprises only do short-term operational planning, and only a few link it to future skill needs. Second, talent recruitment is difficult: the hiring rate for positions in Europe is 56%, 18% of new employees leave during probation, and the overall recruitment success rate is only 46%. Third, employee development is fragmented: 26% of European employees receive no feedback throughout the year, 30% do not participate in training, and only one-third of key positions have succession plans. Fourth, employee experience needs improvement: nearly 20% of employees are dissatisfied with their employer, but the voluntary turnover rate is low, creating a "quiet quitting" risk, and HR has a biased understanding of employee retention factors. Fifth, HR service efficiency needs optimization: only 18% of large enterprises use HR shared service centers, and in Europe only 19% of core HR processes apply generative AI.

To address these issues, the report proposes response strategies. For workforce planning, skill tracking and strategic planning should be combined to build a dynamic skill classification system. For recruitment, a "talent war room" can be established to strengthen internal hiring and use generative AI to improve efficiency. Employee development needs to integrate performance, learning, and talent development, and increase feedback frequency and training practicality. Employee experience optimization should focus on factors employees value, such as job security, and adopt hybrid work models. HR services should promote shared service centers and expand the application of generative AI in processes.

The report emphasizes that HR needs to integrate the above areas, upgrade its operating model with technology, and shift from an administrative function to a strategic partner to improve the organization's long-term performance.
