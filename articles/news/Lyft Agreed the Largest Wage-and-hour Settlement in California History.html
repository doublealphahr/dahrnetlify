Lyft has agreed to pay $272.5 million to settle allegations that it misclassified California drivers as independent contractors rather than employees, depriving them of wage and workplace protections under state law. The settlement, announced on October 1, 2026, is the largest wage-and-hour settlement in California history.

Of the total settlement amount, **at least $237,075,000 (87%)** will be reserved for drivers who worked for Lyft. The remainder covers penalties and administrative costs. The California Labor Commissioner’s Office redirected its share of penalties ($5.45 million) back to drivers, and the more than 1,600 drivers who filed wage claims through the LCO’s administrative process will receive a multiplier that doubles the mileage used to calculate their payment.
