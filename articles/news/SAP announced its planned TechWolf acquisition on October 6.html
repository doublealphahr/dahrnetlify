SAP to Acquire TechWolf, Giving Enterprises Evidence-Based View of Work in the Age of AI

Acquisition details and timing

  • SAP announced its planned TechWolf acquisition on October 6, 2026.
  • The transaction is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026.
  • TechWolf said it had spent eight years building its work-intelligence platform.

SAP SE announced on October 6, 2026, that it had agreed to acquire TechWolf, a provider of an artificial-intelligence work-intelligence platform, according to SAP News Center. The transaction is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to customary closing conditions, including regulatory approval.

Chronology of the week

The October 6 announcement said echWolf’s context graph, artificial-intelligence models and applied research team would become part of SAP’s work-intelligence strategy. SAP said TechWolf is expected to become an intelligent core of the SAP SuccessFactors portfolio, supporting skills mapping, workforce planning and organizational redesign.

TechWolf’s platform combines information about work, employee skills and the external labor market. The company said its open-source models had been downloaded more than two million times. TechWolf CEO Andreas De Neve said the company had spent eight years building its evidence layer for questions about work and workforce needs.

Dates of the coming week

SAP said the transaction is targeted for completion in the fourth quarter of 2026. The release did not disclose financial terms and said completion remains subject to regulatory approval.
